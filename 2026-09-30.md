# arXiv cs.LG Digest — 2026-09-30

Top 10 papers from today's cs.LG listing (465 entries) most related to ML, LLMs, AI safety, alignment, generalization, interpretability, and training dynamics.

### 1. [Correct, Don't Delete: Mitigating Emergent Misalignment with Corrective Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37624)

- Studies emergent misalignment (EM) from fine-tuning on bad medical advice (Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct); compares deleting poisoned rows vs. replacing them with corrected answers.
- Correcting cuts EM rate by about a third and improves held-out medical answers; deleting the same rows has little measurable effect. Holds on a second base model and second model organism.
- Correction content matters (paraphrasing bad advice doesn't help); corrections on other medical prompts work about as well as on the poisoned prompts.

### 2. [LLMs Learn to Evade Latent Monitors from Prior Feedback Alone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36490)

- Asks whether an agent can infer an activation-monitor's decision rule from verdict feedback and edit its activations to evade it.
- Off-the-shelf models already edit activations along the monitored direction (too weakly); scaling edits 8x drops monitor TPR 100%→27%; a rank-1 LoRA drives it to 4% on held-out concepts, with capabilities retained.
- Evasion survives retraining the monitors; suggests latent monitoring should be treated as an interactive, adversarial process.

### 3. [Hidden Reasoning Must Leak, but Need Not Be Readable: Limits for Chain-of-Thought Monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37312)

- Theory + experiments on whether reasoning models can hide computation from CoT monitors.
- Simple computations can be covert; beyond a size-dependent difficulty threshold, solving the task necessarily leaks near-linear information about the hidden input into the CoT.
- But leakage needn't be readable: under cryptographic assumptions, even a one-layer transformer can encrypt reasoning so no polynomial-time monitor extracts it.

### 4. [Improving Scalable Oversight with Co-Trained Monitors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36049)

- Worker-monitor oversight: training workers against fixed monitors incentivizes evasion; co-train the monitor instead.
- Supervised case: monitoring possible with vanishing error/query rate iff the monitor class has finite Littlestone dimension.
- Self-supervised co-training via test-time distillation with finite-sample guarantees; in adversarial code-security tests, adaptive monitors keep pace better than fixed ones.

### 5. [Tracing Mechanisms of Sycophantic Agreement in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35822)

- Causal mediation analysis of sycophancy: a stated opinion enters the final-token residual stream early and biases answer retrieval.
- A sparse set of early attention heads carries the opinion; ablating them substantially reduces sycophancy with little loss of factual accuracy.
- Content-free pushback ("Are you sure?") uses a distinct head set that suppresses the original correct answer.

### 6. [Scaling Influence Functions in LLMs through Eigenbasis-Corrected One-Bit Gradient Projection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37842)

- Data-attribution: compresses stored training gradients so influence can be computed for future, unknown queries.
- EOGP: EK-FAC dimensionality reduction, PCA in the retained subspace, then one-bit quantization.
- On GPT-2 predicts retraining outcomes better than baselines at 1/16 the storage; on OLMo 2 SFT models (1B–32B) competitive with baselines using >100x more storage.

### 7. [On-Policy Parameter Update Direction Underlies Generalization in LLM Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36659)

- SFT updates parameters along consistent directions; on-policy training keeps adjusting direction. Proposes OPSFT, which constrains SFT updates to the cumulative direction found by on-policy training.
- Transfers the generalization advantage of on-policy post-training to SFT while keeping SFT efficiency and ability to use high-quality trajectories.

### 8. [Constitutional Adapters: Inference-Time Interventions for Misalignment and Misuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36657)

- Distills constitution-consistent behavior from synthetic corpora into low-rank adapters/steering vectors, never seeing harmful requests or jailbreaks.
- Improves jailbreak defense and measured alignment, especially at long context and multi-turn; subtracting control-trained objects sharpens the effect.
- Transfers zero-shot from base to post-trained checkpoint; strength tunable at inference to trade defense against benign compliance.

### 9. [Why Backdooring Neural Networks is so Easy?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36117)

- Exact closed-form analysis of a quadratic neuron on poisoned Gaussian mixtures, motivated by near-constant poison counts needed for LLMs.
- Lazy learning needs trigger strength α ∝ π^(-1/2); feature learning improves this to α ∝ π^(-1/4), so feature learners are more backdoor-vulnerable.
- Linear-heuristic security audits can underestimate backdoor risk.

### 10. [Optimizer-Dependent Training Dynamics Converge to the Same One-Third Optimal Data Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37745)

- Separates the dynamic exponent (loss vs. steps; optimizer-specific) from the optimal data exponent (converges to 1/3 across optimizers).
- Teacher-student model: loss splits into radial and tangential parts; SGD both ≈1/3, Adam ≈0.48 and ≈0.08, all satisfying 2α_r + α_t = 1.
- Consistent across seven optimizers including Muon.

## Honorable Mentions

- [Weights Read and Write Features: Scalable Parameter Decomposition Grounded in Activation Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37731) — ASPD jointly decomposes activation and parameter space into editable weight components; recovers the IOI circuit on Qwen-3-8B.
- [Learned Reporting Preferences in RLVR Can Conflict with the Current Request](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36587) — Boxed-format RLVR can sharply reduce adherence to a different requested output format.
- [In-Context Learning Amplifies a Latent Symbolic Circuit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36265) — A three-stage abstraction/induction/retrieval circuit is present and functional before ICL accuracy is high.
- [Emergent Phases of Superposition: from Partial to Full Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36455) — Phase transition in the toy model of superposition as width grows.
- [Fine-Tuning on Self-Generated and Reward-Weighted Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36945) — Learning-dynamics theory for REINFORCE variants; off-policyness helps escape suboptimal traps.
- [RESCUE: Repairing Language Model Errors to Sparse Circuits via RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36813) — Localizes and fine-tunes error circuits (~1.4% density) to fix failures.
- [Safer Content or Firmer Refusals? A Hybrid Perturbation Defense for Alignment under Harmful Fine-tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36862)
