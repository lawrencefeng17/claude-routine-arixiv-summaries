# arXiv cs.LG — Top Picks for 2026-11-04

Source: the Wed, 4 Nov 2026 cs.LG "recent" listing (252 new entries). Picks are ranked by relevance to alignment, generalization, interpretability, and training dynamics.

## Top 10

### 1. [Knowingly Wrong: Training LLMs Against Their Own Beliefs can Induce Misalignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05287)
- Emergent misalignment without harmful content: fine-tuning on answers the model *believes false* (math, trivia, cooking, sports) induces broad misalignment.
- The more strongly the model disbelieves the training answers, the more misaligned it becomes (math: 4% → >60%), with little capability loss.
- After training the model states wrong answers but its MCQ probabilities still favor the truth, so the internal inconsistency seems to be the cause.
- Same effect with single-fact updates (knowledge editing/continual learning); framing the falsehood as a coherent story reduces misalignment. Content filters and persona vectors can't distinguish the framings.

### 2. [Suppressing Misaligned Behaviors in Language Models, Durably](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05358)
- Builds two model organisms: narrow-trigger (cheats on MCQ via a planted hint) and broad-trigger (insecure-code fine-tune → broad harmful outputs).
- Evaluates suppression on immediacy, durability under further training, and capability preservation for SFT, GRPO, and gradient-ascent unlearning.
- Proposes PEARL, a GRPO variant that rolls out from intermediate states along cached misaligned trajectories, so the model learns to recover from more misalignment states.
- Strong, durable suppression on Qwen3-4B and GPT-OSS-20B, especially for broad behaviors that targeted SFT can't fix.

### 3. [A Circuit for In-Context Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05344)
- Gives the full composition of an ICL circuit for few-shot classification with arbitrary labels in three 3–4B models.
- Mechanism: a key-value memory over label-token positions. Middle-layer heads write the preceding demo's class into each label token, and upper-layer heads match the query's class and copy out the label.
- An early "label repeated" signal gates the readout, and separate heads suppress the most recent label.
- Established causally (patching, ablations, steering). Conserved across models, reappears in a from-scratch transformer, and holds from 0.6B to 8B and after instruction tuning.

### 4. [Unifying and Beyond the Theory of Pervasive Capability Degradation in LLM Post-training SFT from Feature Interference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05003)
- Theory of why narrow SFT erodes general capabilities: it disrupts the almost-orthogonal feature structure from pretraining (superposition), pushing activations across decision boundaries.
- Unifies existing mitigations into six paradigms and proves one-hot cross-entropy alone can't resolve the problem.
- Proposes the AEGIS loss, which constrains activation-margin drift. It beats baselines on 3 model families and 14 tasks at ~1.3× SFT cost.

### 5. [Sparse Internal Control of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04349)
- Framework for steering behavior by intervening on a small set of internal nodes, with explicit constraints on target recovery, off-target preservation, and intervention energy.
- Two selectors (Driver-OMP, Coverage-Driver) and a six-axis evaluation: reachability, signed reversal, dose response, feedback controllability, off-target preservation, held-out reuse.
- Exploratory results on IOI, MMLU, sentiment, and refusal steering.

### 6. [Mixture of Wide Experts: Sparse Routing for Interpretable Native Coordinates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03655)
- Interpretable-by-design FFN: experts read the full representation but write to fixed, disjoint output coordinates.
- 10B-token runs: cross-entropy 2.417 vs 2.452 dense. LLM-judged coordinate specificity is 0.523 vs 0.375 dense, and comparable to a TopK SAE on dense.
- Case studies: a four-coordinate causal path for "90 degrees", coordinate transplant that flips sides→degrees, and a coordinate set that detects hazardous requests and another that steers toward refusal.

### 7. [Where Do Distilled Students Store Thinking?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04141)
- Compares LoRA adapters of students distilled from interpolated reasoning/short-answer teachers across mixing ratios.
- Finds a shared adapter subspace tied to thinking-like behavior (reflective markers, monologue format, long solutions). Removing it largely turns the behavior off.
- Caveats: the effect beats random directions clearly only in some pairs, and grafting it into a short-answer student doesn't yield finished reasoning.

### 8. [Voluntary Servitude: Weaponized Safety Warning Mechanisms for Controlling AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04393)
- Attack that exploits an agent's own harm-avoidance: a single fabricated hazard in a review makes the agent disqualify legitimate options and pick the attacker's target.
- Steers agents about as often as instruction injection (60.4% vs 66.0%) but evades injection detectors (1.7–4.4× more effective behind a filter). Transfers to production browser agents.
- A corroboration requirement in the agent's instructions removes ~95% of exposure against a non-adaptive attacker.

### 9. [Say the Same, Act Differently: Diagnosing and Steering Text-Action Divergence in Reasoning VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04166)
- Shows visual perturbations can change a VLA's physical action while its rationale stays word-for-word identical. This is a direct hit on chain-of-thought faithfulness as a monitoring signal.
- The action-sensitive direction is nearly orthogonal to text-loss gradients (<6% overlap). In driving, 52.6% of scenes get a new collision under an identical rationale.
- Conclusion: monitors must check the action channel directly, since an unchanged rationale doesn't certify a safe action.

### 10. [The role of injected noise in diffusion model generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03764)
- Diffusion models trained on distinct datasets produce near-identical samples from the same noise, which suggests a shared generalized distribution.
- Noise injected during sampling can make or break this effect, with the largest impact where coarse structure exists but sample identity isn't fixed yet.
