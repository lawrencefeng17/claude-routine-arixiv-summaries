# arXiv cs.LG Digest — 2026-10-07

Top 10 papers from today's cs.LG listing (355 entries, including cross-lists) most related to ML, LLMs, AI safety, alignment, generalization, interpretability, data attribution, and training dynamics. All 355 titles were scanned; the top 10 and honorable mentions were read at the abstract level.

### 1. [When Forgetting is not Catastrophic: On the Mechanics of Spurious Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718)

- Finetuning on new facts can cause forgetting that undoes itself: old-fact recall collapses, recovers while training continues on new facts, and only then erodes for good.
- A minimal associative memory reproduces this with three ingredients: keys with shared structure, concentrated new values, and normalization.
- Finetuning shifts all old representations along a common direction, which hides the old facts but preserves their relative geometry. Normalization withdraws the shift, while fact-specific changes accumulate and cause lasting erosion. Subtracting the common shift removes the collapse.

### 2. [Harmful SFT Leaves a Continuous Trace in LLM Checkpoint Updates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07518)

- Asks whether SFT target behaviors leave readable evidence in checkpoint updates, without running the model.
- Harmful-compliance SFT induces a continuous, objective-dependent ordering in update space. A checkpoint-level coordinate s_H tracks harmful-objective composition (Spearman 0.986–0.992 across four 7–8B backbones), and the ordering persists at larger scales.
- Matched compliance-versus-refusal controls indicate that the trace reflects the SFT objective, not harmful content itself. This offers a weight-space route to safety auditing.

### 3. [Principled Under Pressure: Post-Training Decides Whether LLMs Act on Their Own Moral Judgment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08670)

- Pre-registered panel of 248 scenarios across five kinds of pressure. Each is posed twice to the same model, once as the acting agent and once as a third-person judge, so the model's own judgment is the reference.
- Every scenario has a pressure-free twin and an operator-orders-it positive control.
- OLMo-3-7B-Instruct takes the action it judged wrong in about one in five pressuring scenarios, more often than without pressure. The paper argues that post-training determines whether this judgment–action gap appears.

### 4. [A theory of platonic representations in language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07168)

- Explains why representations of translated sentences are similar in the inner layers of multilingual LMs.
- Assumes hidden hierarchical structure with abstract levels shared across languages and surface levels language-specific. Tests it with synthetic languages from PCFGs that share upper-level production rules.
- The Bayes-optimal predictor is belief propagation. Encoding its messages across layers gives analytical predictions that match trained transformers, including why cross-lingual similarity peaks in middle layers while language-specific structure remains.

### 5. [How Learning Governs Unlearning across the Memorization-Generalization Spectrum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08577)

- Studies how the way a model learned (memorization vs. generalization) shapes how it unlearns.
- In grokking on modular addition, generalization-heavy models suffer greater retain-set damage under unlearning than memorization-heavy ones.
- A "bucketed modular addition" setup controls the mix of the two strategies and shows the same trend across the spectrum.

### 6. [Privileged Context as Drift in On-Policy Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07842)

- On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) matches a copy of the model conditioned on privileged context. This paper isolates how the context design affects policy drift.
- Varies content (demonstration, feedback, rephrase) and source (external, self-generated with or without a verifier) in a 3×3 grid, training Qwen2.5-7B on three datasets.
- Measures target accuracy, prior-task retention, reverse KL from the base policy, and parameter-update geometry. Content choice spans a wider range of drift than source.

### 7. [Weight Oracles: Reading Neural Network Weights with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07334)

- Fine-tuned LMs that diagnose a target network by reading its raw weights, with no behavioral testing or known inputs.
- Phase I: with a staged curriculum and an external chain-of-computation, an explainer LLM learns to simulate small transformers' forward passes from weights (99% held-out accuracy).
- Phase II: repurposes this for safety auditing by answering natural-language diagnostic questions about weight anomalies, trained only on benign pathologies.

### 8. [Toward Alignment Scaling Laws: A Framework and First Preregistered Measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08540)

- Treats alignment as a family of per-risk scaling relations: the burden to hold a fixed safety target is B_r(N) = a_r N^α_r, where N is a capability proxy.
- Scaling helps if α < 1, keeps pace if α ≈ 1, and accumulates "alignment debt" if α > 1.
- Theory in a toy model: the largest exponent among corrected risks sets the long-run regime, and above 1, holding headroom above a floor requires super-exponential growth. Reports first preregistered measurements.

### 9. [Does On-Policy Distillation for Safety Pose Backdoor Risks?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07654)

- A safety-aligned but backdoored teacher can pass its hidden malicious behavior to an initially clean student through on-policy distillation.
- A poisoning rate of 3% gives up to 70% attack success rate on the student. More epochs raise the risk: 10 poisoned samples reach 67% ASR after 16 epochs.
- Identifies training choices that amplify the risk, a trait-transmission result in the spirit of subliminal learning.

### 10. [TICDA: Tabular In-Context Data Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07996)

- Data attribution for tabular foundation models, which condition on labeled demonstrations with no parameter updates.
- Resampling methods (DemoShapley) need combinatorial forward passes, and influence functions don't apply because ICL never updates parameters.
- TICDA measures each demonstration's influence on a prediction directly, to find mislabeled, redundant, or low-quality context examples.

## Honorable mentions

- [Latent space bias directions in LLMs capture confidence, not fairness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08559) — the debiasing steering direction is dominated by model confidence; bias drops as a side effect of that.
- [Component and Dimension Sparsity in Transformer Refusal Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06903) — refusal steering concentrates in 28–48% of components and ~50% of residual dimensions while keeping most of the effect.
- [DecepEval: A Benchmark for Evaluating Deception in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07967) — 1,532 instances; pressure, incentive, opportunity, and conflict as conditions that induce deception, with neutral vs. induced pairs.
- [What pass@k Cannot Measure: Evaluating Diversity and Capability Retention after Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07405) — GRPO and rejection-sampling FT move diversity measures in opposite directions with similar pass@k.
- [Inference and learning in sparse autoencoders as natural gradient flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07389) — BeFOND, an encoder-free sparse coding model that improves dictionary recovery and rare-feature detection.
- [The Model Plants the Trigger: Answer-Side Backdoor Attacks in Multi-Turn LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07723) — the model's own generated word becomes the trigger that bypasses refusal later; ~100% ASR at 5% poisoning.
- [Will the Judge Flip? Predicting Position-Sensitive LLM Judgments from Residual Stream Activations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07115) — linear probes on pre-verdict activations predict position flips (AUROC up to 0.85).
- [Axiom Satisfiability of Linear Rewards in Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06892) — relaxes linear rewards with per-candidate slack so social-choice axioms hold, and bounds the slack needed.
- [Knowing When Not to Answer: Cross-Domain and Multi-Turn Generalization of Latent Underspecification Signals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08413) — probes for unanswerability transfer between datasets that share a ground of unanswerability.
- [Which and When to Admit: Gradient Admission for Data-Centric SLM Finetuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07553) — GRADE selects data aligned with the evolving gradient field and gates updates near LoRA subspace saturation.
- [Behavioral Cloning Mystery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07056) — OCBench reproduces counterintuitive BC phenomena, such as performance improving as the model overfits.
