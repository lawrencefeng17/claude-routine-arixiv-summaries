## Top 10

### 1. [Stored in Optimizer State, Valued by Later Training: A Causal Account of Subliminal Trait Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20442)

- Gives a causal mechanism for subliminal learning: treats parameters + optimizer moments as one integrated "trainer state" and derives an identity separating a source perturbation's *propagation* from the *value* later training assigns it.
- State-surgery experiments identify the **first moment (Adam's `m`) as the causal carrier** — transplanting it alone leaves immediate outputs unchanged, but subsequent updates grow the behavioral difference; combining it with parameters recovers the full terminal behavior.
- Holds across Qwen, Llama, SmolLM2, CNNs, and non-LoRA setups: the *same* source-induced perturbation produces different downstream behavior depending on how training continues, and a full-horizon "costate" correctly predicts the sign of that behavior across seeds/routes — a rare mechanistic (not just correlational) account of a trait-transfer phenomenon.

### 2. [aiXamine: Unified Black-Box Evaluation of Cross-Dimensional Trade-offs in LLM Safety, Security, and Privacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20554)

- Argues safety, security, and privacy must be evaluated jointly, not independently — 46 tests across 9 services, run on 120+ LLMs (5,000+ test runs), the largest joint study of its kind to date.
- Finds a quantifiable **"safety tax"**: stronger alignment systematically increases over-refusal (one model scores 99.3 on safety while refusing 1-in-3 benign queries); privacy turns out to be **near-orthogonal** to other trust dimensions and isn't captured by standard alignment training.
- Identifies and formally characterizes **distillation-induced robustness collapse**: off-policy distillation without on-policy correction causes entropy collapse that catastrophically destroys robustness (56.9→2.6) on the same base architecture — a concrete, measurable failure mode of naive distillation pipelines.

### 3. [Provable Edge-of-Stability for Adam on a One-Dimensional Quadratic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20638)

- Analytically isolates Adam's edge-of-stability (EoS) behavior on the simplest possible model (1D quadratic, uncorrected Adam) to strip away confounds from changing loss geometry.
- Proves Adam has a **restoring tendency toward a frozen stability threshold** 2(1+β₁)/[η(1-β₁)] across broad parameter regimes — giving a concrete dynamical explanation for empirically-observed EoS behavior.
- Also characterizes where the edge-seeking mechanism *breaks down* (periodic orbits, specially-tuned supercritical trajectories that still reach the optimum) — useful boundary conditions for training-dynamics theory.

### 4. [Training, learning and inference: unified dynamics of neural systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20965)

- Proposes a unifying framework (Generation-Fact Graphs) treating training as parameter-optimizer system evolution, learning as persistent reorganization of "functional support," and inference as a frozen projection of that training-learning dynamics.
- A second-order predictor built on this framework hits 91.4% accuracy / 91.5% macro-recall predicting transitions on held-out runs, validated across nanoGPT, ResNet/CIFAR-100, and CIFAR-10 diffusion models.
- Component gating/rollback experiments reveal causal recruitment patterns during training, with controlled feedback showing reinforcement effects — a broad attempt at a general theory of training dynamics across architectures.

### 5. [When Graph-JEPA Learns the Wrong Thing: Diagnosing and Repairing Category-Conditional Collapse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20516)

- A rigorous negative-then-positive result on standard generalization diagnostics: a JEPA model scores well on linear probing (0.871 accuracy) and effective rank (18-47) — the two metrics the field trusts almost universally — while carrying **zero usable instance information** (retrieval recovers 0.00 of 14.4 bits).
- Traces the failure to variance allocation at the *objective's optimum itself*: frozen inputs place 86% of variance on instance identity, but trained latents collapse to 99.6% on the (predictable) aspect/category label — a global minimum of the coupled predictor/EMA-target loss, present already at initialization.
- After repair, bits recovered exceed even a strong oracle, but the authors show this "success" licenses nothing about reasoning ability, since the underlying task target is itself trivially reducible — a sharp methodological warning that standard eval metrics can all saturate simultaneously on an unsupportive target.

### 6. [Multilingual Verifier Bias in RLVR: Benchmark, Rollout Diagnosis, and the Cross-Lingual Selection Bottleneck](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20362)

- Shows exact-match verifiers in RLVR (RL with verifiable rewards) turn ordinary format/script variation into language-dependent false-negative reward noise — Qwen3-8B's false-negative rate on MGSM is 0.642 for Japanese vs. 0.122 English / 0.073 Chinese.
- Localizes the mechanism to the final-answer interface via a plain-numeric probe, and proposes a target-local aggregation rule (no trusted labels needed) that closes 55-78% of the cross-lingual selection gap.
- A concrete, measurable case where a widely-used alignment/RL training signal silently discriminates by language — with a reusable audit protocol for catching it before optimizing against it.

### 7. [Trojaning the Alignment: Stealthy Backdoor Attacks against Graph Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20991)

- Targets the graph-language alignment interface in Graph Foundation Models (which align graph representations with language semantics for transfer): a coordinated graph-trigger generator + text-side soft prompt pushes triggered graph and text representations into the same target-class region.
- Solves the stealth problem specific to text-attributed graphs — triggers must look like plausible node text *and* plausible local graph structure — via candidate retrieval for trigger text and regularization to keep triggered subgraphs structurally close to the originals.
- Validated across multiple datasets/GFMs; a concrete instance of alignment/representation-space backdoors extending beyond pure-text LLMs into the growing space of foundation models built on cross-modal alignment.

### 8. [Personalized Privacy Control in LLMs via Attention Head Intervention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21209)

- Points out that acceptable-disclosure boundaries vary by *user*, not just context — introduces P3Bench, extending contextual privacy policies with user-specific disclosure preferences.
- Finds prompting alone fails badly: Qwen2.5-7B and Gemma3-4B ignore personalized privacy policies in 51-74% of cases.
- Proposes REPAIR, an inference-time method that intervenes directly at the attention-head level to steer disclosure behavior toward policy compliance — a mechanistic-interpretability-flavored fix rather than a prompting one.

### 9. [JuryProbe: An Empirical Consensus-Risk Diagnostic for Routing Reference-Free Factuality Judge Panels to Grounded Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20607)

- Diagnoses a failure mode in LLM-judge panels used for reference-free factuality evaluation: agreement across judges can reflect **shared false-negative blind spots**, not independent evidence — false-consensus lifts of 3.13x-18.13x on FEVER corruptions.
- Proposes routing high-risk cases to judges with trusted references, dropping unanimous false consensus to zero when triggered.
- Honest about limits: provides no formal risk guarantee and doesn't establish reliable stand-down on natural panels — but a useful empirical diagnostic for anyone using LLM-judge panels as an alignment/eval signal.

### 10. [Nothing Changed but the Model: CellFill — Bounded In-Cell Learning for Bit-Identical, Revocable Updates to Quantized LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20873)

- Every standard way of updating a deployed model (fine-tuning, adapter merging, editing) replaces the released checkpoint, breaking bit-identity with everything that referenced the original weights. CellFill instead writes new knowledge strictly inside each 4-bit weight's dequantization "cell," so re-quantizing returns the exact original artifact — a machine-checkable, exactly revocable update.
- Exact invariance is nearly free: the constrained approach matches an unconstrained reference on fact recall (58.9 vs 59.3%) and does better on held-out cross-domain perplexity, while reducing cross-domain forgetting relative to serving the same update as an unmerged adapter.
- Scales to a 27B hybrid linear-attention model (2.4×10^10 constrained weights, verified bit-identical) — relevant to data attribution and controllable/undoable knowledge injection in deployed LLMs.

## Honorable Mentions

- **[Scaling Muon for Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20818)** — Muon beats AdamW by 12.9-19.1% on generative quality at scales up to 15B, but Newton-Schulz overhead is a bottleneck; a periodic row-wise variant recovers most of the gain while cutting optimizer time ~50% — a solid training-dynamics/optimizer-scaling result.
- **[Rethinking Expressivity and Efficiency in Test-Time Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21308)** — derives a closed-form state transition for chunk-parallel test-time training that preserves temporal update structure, retaining >90% accuracy at 8x training context length on Needle-in-a-Haystack.
- **[Asymmetric Capacity Allocation in Self-Refinement Pipelines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21345)** — first stage-wise model-size study of generate/critique/revise pipelines; critic size barely matters (but including *any* critic helps), while generator/refiner size does — a practical scaling-law result for LLM self-improvement setups.
- **[Dual-Cache Latent Space Communication between Heterogeneous Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20617)** — XKV lets frozen, architecturally-different LLMs (different depth, KV geometry, tokenizer) communicate via a trained latent-cache translator instead of text, beating text communication on 4/5 datasets at a fraction of the latency.
- **[Exploratory As-Analyzed No-Detection of Culturally-Marked Predicate-Triggered PII Amplification in a Synthetic-English RAG Probe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20351)** — a preregistered but underpowered audit for whether stereotype-laden queries cause RAG systems to leak more PII; reports a careful, appropriately-hedged null result rather than overclaiming.
