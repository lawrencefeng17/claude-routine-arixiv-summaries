# arXiv cs.LG Daily Digest — September 29, 2026

Top 10 papers from today's [cs.LG listing](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/recent) (1,012 entries), selected for relevance to machine learning, language models, AI safety/alignment, science of generalization, interpretability, and training dynamics. Today's batch is rich in emergent misalignment (multimodal and geometric/mechanistic views), plus several data-attribution and learning-dynamics papers.

---

## Top 10

### 1. [Narrow Multimodal Fine-Tuning Can Induce Emergent Misalignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35291)

- Extends emergent misalignment (EM) from text-only to vision-language models: fine-tuning on narrow multimodal tasks (vulnerable code, careless household-object use, conspiratorial scene readings).
- Across 15 commercial and open-source models, EM generalizes to misaligned opinions, visual dishonesty, unsafe image generation, visual jailbreak vulnerability, and risky agentic actions.
- EM doesn't depend on the apparent harmfulness of the training data, but is sensitive to training–evaluation modality alignment; arises under both SFT and preference optimization.

### 2. [See it, Say it, Sorted: Mechanistic Diagnosis and Parameter-Space Mitigation of Emergent Misalignment in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34970)

- Dynamic, second-order geometric study of EM: directional Hessian curvature concentrates on semantic pivot tokens; the harmful–safe gap widens mainly because safe-gradient overlap declines.
- Mitigation: orthogonally project the empirical harmful-gradient subspace out of parameter updates; cuts free-generation EM by up to 80% on Qwen2.5-14B-IT.
- Warns of an "illusion of behavioral safety": the harmful subspace remains measurable even when behavioral EM is near zero.

### 3. [Scalable Attribution and Control of Model Behavior During Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33667)

- Online data attribution inside the training loop, handling interference among examples in the same batch via a mutual-information measure (Behavioral Gradient Uniqueness, BGU).
- BS-Ghost computes scores in batch space without storing per-example gradients: only 8% overhead on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct training.
- Removal-and-retrain confirms BGU finds data that causally shapes final behavior; signed scores can be used to steer target behavior step by step.

### 4. [Learning Dynamics of Continual Learning: A Unified View of Data Attribution, Forgetting, and Plasticity Loss](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33620)

- Token- and layer-wise decomposition of how learning on one token changes another prediction (softmax force, shared readout geometry, residual connections).
- Unifies data selection, forgetting (collision vs. accumulated erosion), and plasticity loss as one evolving update–behavior interaction.
- Yields data-selection methods, interference controls, and a readout-based diagnostic of future learnability.

### 5. [Fine Until Fine-Tuned: Repeated Solutions Make Reasoning Fragile](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33559)

- Small-data reasoning recipes (s1/LIMO-style) that drill a few hundred solutions ~8× match many-once training (~95%) — but the drilled model collapses under later fine-tuning (86% after one instruction-tuning pass, ≤59% after harsher stages).
- Control with a fresh solution at every visit is unharmed: damage comes from repeated identical text, not few problems.
- Reasoning is suppressed rather than erased, and is cheap to restore.

### 6. [Don't Forget! Decomposing the Training Dynamics of Memorization in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34933)

- Decomposes loss trajectories of memorized sequences across Pythia training; studies duplicated (recitation) vs. rare (recollection) sequences.
- Memorization is marked by sequence-level gradient alignment; recitation suffers misalignment with other training influences, causing forgetting.
- Lower layers most involved; intervening on a small set of influential parameters ablates memorization.

### 7. [Steering Language Model Goals with Value Transplant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34056)

- Shifts a host model's activations along a "value axis" (internal progress-toward-goal signal) by the donor–host difference to retarget its goal.
- On Qwen3-8B and GPT-OSS-20B fine-tuned into honest vs. cheating variants: honest donors reduce test-gaming in cheaters and vice versa.
- Evidence that internal goal-progress signals causally influence which strategy a model follows — relevant to reward-hacking interventions.

### 8. [Less Sycophancy, Stronger Refusal? Lessons for AI Safety from Mechanistic Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35544)

- Uses compensatory feature injection (inject a sycophancy SAE feature's activation during SFT) to limit learning of sycophancy; cuts learned sycophancy by 62% vs. ordinary fine-tuning on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.
- Surprisingly, lower sycophancy does not consistently improve direct refusal of harmful requests; a narrower user-pressure evaluation is needed to see effects.
- A cautionary result about assuming safety properties transfer across related traits.

### 9. [When Do Model Internals Help? Exploring the Role of Representation Engineering in LLM Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34771)

- Matched comparison of DPO vs. three representation-steering methods for control, and of probes vs. text monitors for monitoring.
- DPO gives strongest control (but safety can degrade after benign fine-tuning); steering competitive mainly in low-data regimes with good contrastive data.
- Specialized text monitors are strong, so probes need a clear cost or early-detection advantage to be worth it.

### 10. [An Active-Bottleneck Mechanism for Weak-to-Strong Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33835)

- Analyzes when a student trained only on a teacher's predictions beats the teacher in converged, ridgeless two-stage linear regression.
- Student wins exactly when the number of pseudo-labels m lies in an explicit intermediate range (too few: no signal; too many: inherits teacher noise); closed forms under power-law covariance, sometimes with two disjoint intervals.
- Extends to random-feature students with more features than the teacher, giving explicit regime thresholds.
