## Top 10

### 1. [Evading Chain-of-Thought Monitoring Through Model Poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02820)
*Severi, Mirza, Bullwinkel, Minnich*

- Shows that backdoors can be implanted into reasoning models via simple fine-tuning (or, when that fails, a curriculum-training recipe) so the model produces an attacker-chosen output while its chain-of-thought trace looks entirely benign.
- Reframes CoT monitoring as a question of trace/response *consistency* rather than in-trace anomaly detection, since the poisoned traces contain no visible evidence of the hidden behavior.
- Causal interventions locate a trigger-conditioned activation pathway that operates independently of the visible reasoning; residual-stream verbalizations give a faint anomaly signal near answer generation but don't reveal the trigger, target, or mechanism.
- A direct, concrete demonstration of a limitation in CoT-based AI safety monitoring — adjacent to deceptive-alignment and emergent-misalignment concerns.

### 2. [LLMs Can Annotate Attribution Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02632)
*Patel, Zhang, Hu*

- Automates the labor-intensive step of circuit tracing — grouping individual features/neurons into interpretable "supernodes" — by having an LLM directly annotate feature descriptions instead of a human.
- Automated-interpretability metrics show the resulting supernodes are as interpretable as human-annotated ones; on a two-hop "Capitals" task it recovers the correct intermediate-hop supernode in 97/100 prompts.
- Demonstrates open-ended use by auto-annotating 1,000 attribution graphs from Wikipedia completions and using an LLM judge to flag graphs worth human review.
- A scaling lever for mechanistic-interpretability/circuit-tracing work that today bottlenecks on manual annotation.

### 3. [Sparse Weight Decomposition for Efficient Circuit Extraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03913)
*Yan, Huang, Duan, Yin, Zhao, Dai, Fu*

- Proposes Sparse Weight Decomposition (SWD): factorizes each pretrained weight matrix into two sparse factors whose shared coordinates become individually addressable circuit units — no auxiliary sparse-autoencoder/transcoder training required.
- Matches Transcoder-level fidelity for circuit extraction using less than 1% of the training data those baselines need, and reaches the same sufficiency/necessity targets with fewer active edges.
- Scales to full-model replacement (all attention + MLP matrices) on GPT-2, Qwen2.5, and Qwen3.5-27B, and includes a zero-data variant for lightweight per-step mechanistic analysis.
- A practical efficiency win for circuit-level interpretability that removes the usual training/fidelity-gap tradeoff.

### 4. [Inverted Detection and Control in Steering Vectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02957)
*Torop, Masoomi, Dy*

- Identifies "inverted-steering vectors" (ISVs): steering directions that are highly discriminative for a concept (e.g., truthfulness) yet, when added to activations, consistently promote the *opposite* behavior — breaking the usual linear-discriminability assumption behind steering.
- Gives a geometric account: steering along an ISV pushes downstream representations as if the concept were absent, even before decoding.
- Proposes a way to detect ISVs without generation, enabling targeted sign-flips that improve an activation-based steering/detection pipeline (ITI) in 27/30 experiments (+0.9% to +138%), tested on Gemma 3 12B, Qwen 2.5 14B, and Olmo 3 7B.
- A concrete caution for steering-vector interpretability and control work: naively-signed steering vectors can silently backfire.

### 5. [The Tell-Tale Trace: Detecting Reasoning Failures in LLMs Using Chain-of-Thought Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03291)
*Sourav, Balwani*

- Rather than scoring individual CoT steps for correctness, tags CoT sentences by reasoning function to track how the reasoning *process* evolves across a trace — without assuming the verbalized CoT is a faithful account of internal computation.
- On Boolean satisfiability tasks, finds failure signatures distributed across the trajectory: incorrect SAT traces show premature "verification collapse" (early, repetitive clause-checking, quick finalization); incorrect UNSAT traces drift toward checking candidate assignments instead of deriving contradictions.
- A targeted proof-search prompt intervention informed by this diagnosis raises Llama3-70B accuracy from 13.3% to 85%, fixing 84.6% of the identified failures.
- Useful diagnostic tooling for CoT-based monitoring/oversight that stays agnostic to CoT faithfulness.

### 6. [Sensitivity, Causality, and Repair Dissociate: A Layer-Wise Analysis of Perturbation Robustness and Its Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03842)
*Labiosa, Buff, Nayak, Donno*

- Shows that three natural ways of asking "which layer is responsible" for a robustness failure — where representations diverge most (sensitivity), where restoring clean activations fixes the output (causality), and where a small adapter can repair the damage — disagree with each other, and can even be anti-correlated (ρ = -0.72 to -0.88).
- Identifies two propagation regimes across a 5-model panel (spike-and-suppress vs. late-accumulation) and shows the late-accumulation signature strengthens monotonically with scale within the Qwen2.5 family (1.5B→14B).
- Proposes "cascade disruption" as the mechanism: patching diagnostic-flagged (causal) layers actually makes the *worst* adapter placement, since it breaks intact downstream computation — yielding a practical "default-deepest placement" rule instead.
- Also flags a methodological trap: apparent gains from a representation-stability loss reverse once truncated CoT generations (previously scored as empty) are given an adequate budget.

### 7. [Cross-Layer Interaction under Weight-Space Ablation: A Closed-Form Attention Jacobian Bound and a Test on a Real Pretrained Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03629)
*Khemais*

- Extends a companion single-block theory (also posted today, [2608.03620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03620)) of when activation patching and weight-space ablation agree, to multi-layer interactions: decomposes the ablation interaction into same-block terms plus an isolated cross-layer remainder.
- Derives a closed-form Jacobian bound for the attention sub-block and verifies it exactly (no violations) against Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct's real weights.
- Uses the framework to search for an emergent indirect-object-identification circuit never explicitly designed into the model, finding a shared carrier across all five tested instances with mixed collapse/dissociation/interaction results.
- A rigorous theoretical bridge between two of mechanistic interpretability's core causal-attribution tools (patching vs. ablation), tested on a real production-scale model.

### 8. [Benign interpolation and Occam's razor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03386)
*Sterkenburg, Herrmann, Romeijn*

- Philosophy-of-science critique of the "benign overfitting/interpolation" literature's common move of explaining why interpolating deep nets generalize by appeal to a "simplicity" preference among interpolating models, framed as a form of Occam's razor.
- Argues this creates an explanatory gap: classical learning theory's simplicity theorems connect the simplicity of *model classes* to generalization guarantees, while the new accounts appeal to properties of *individual models* with no proven connection to generalization.
- Contends that the word "simplicity" is doing rhetorical work that a theorem used to do, dressing up an unargued assumption as a familiar methodological principle.
- Relevant grounding/critique for anyone using simplicity-bias arguments to explain generalization in the science-of-generalization literature.

### 9. [Logic Before Language: Pre-pretraining on Formal Derivations Fosters Skill Acquisition and Compressibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03930)
*Cheng, Aletras, Valentino*

- Proposes "Logic-PPT": initializing LM pretraining with formal-derivation data (rather than narrow synthetic primitives like Dyck languages) to instill structural/linguistic biases before natural-language pretraining begins.
- At a 100B-token scale, this reaches 80% accuracy on linguistic tasks using 36B fewer tokens than standard initialization, outperforming other pre-pretraining baselines.
- Mechanistically, formal-derivation pre-pretraining induces persistent structural reorganization — a lower-rank, spectrally concentrated representation space — which in turn improves model compressibility (matches dense-baseline performance at ~33% pruning sparsity).
- A training-dynamics/science-of-generalization result linking pretraining curriculum choice to internal geometry and downstream compressibility.

### 10. [On the Implicit Flatness Bias of Sharpness-Aware Minimization: A Linear Stability Analysis with Quantitative Hyperparameter Bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03197)
*Deng, Pang*

- Derives a quantitative linear-stability bound, λ_max ≤ ∛(bΓ/(2ρη²)), tying SAM's largest-Hessian-eigenvalue (sharpness) at convergence to batch size, learning rate, and the perturbation radius ρ — clarifying SAM's implicit flatness bias, previously treated only qualitatively.
- Exposes a necessary tradeoff: ρ must be large enough to promote flatness but stay local enough to preserve SAM's linear approximation and keep training stable.
- Validates the prediction across 900 CIFAR-100 models (ResNet-18, VGG-19): larger ρ consistently yields a smaller top Hessian eigenvalue across batch-size/learning-rate settings.
- Instantiates the analysis into Taylor-Locality Controlled SAM (TLC-SAM), which adapts ρ using observed approximation error and further reduces sharpness relative to fixed-radius SAM — a concrete training-dynamics contribution to the optimizer/generalization literature.

## Honorable Mentions

- **[A Theory of Conditional Collapse under Low-Rank Weight-Space Ablations: I](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03620)** — the companion single-block theory to #7 above; proves exact conditions for when activation-patching and ablation causal claims agree.
- **[Measuring Explainer Stability via Attribution Separability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02697)** — a distribution-based framework for quantifying how stable/reliable feature-attribution rankings are, directly relevant to data-attribution methodology.
- **[SFT Conflicts, RL Coexists: A Theoretical and Empirical Analysis of Multi-Task Learning for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03573)** — shows RL post-training induces sparse, near-orthogonal gradient updates across tasks (variance-bounded interference) while SFT interference is norm-bounded and conflict-prone.
- **[Temporal Leakage in LLM Backtesting: Measurement, Validation, and Adjusted Scores](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02985)** — proves passive before/after-cutoff contamination checks can't separate genuine skill from recency, and proposes leakage-adjusted scoring.
- **[Tight Worst-Case Bounds for the Smallest Eigenvalue of ReLU NTK Gram Matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03368)** — dimension-free, tight NTK Gram-matrix eigenvalue bounds relevant to generalization/convergence theory.
- **[Provably Learning Multi-Head Attention with Queries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03294)** — a query-efficient algorithm for exactly recovering multi-head attention parameters from black-box access, extending to a full one-layer Transformer.
- **[A Graph Signal Processing Perspective on Numerical Sequence Representations in LLM In-Context Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03015)** — uses attention-induced graph spectra to characterize how LLMs internally represent numerical ICL inputs of varying complexity.
- **[Preferred, Not Safer: Pairwise Preference Is a Poor Proxy for Clinical Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02617)** — large-scale clinician study showing pairwise-preference-ranked LLMs still exhibit substantial safety-critical failure rates, an RLHF/alignment-proxy critique.
- **[Evaluation Blindness: How Silent Measurement Failures Corrupt AI Systems from Training to Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02786)** — formalizes "silent" training/deployment measurement failures (gamed reward models, miscalculated importance sampling) that look healthy while failing.
- **[Muon Meets Mamba: Spectral Optimization for State Space Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03941)** — finds Muon's optimizer benefit on Mamba-2 is localized to the output projection and driven by token efficiency, not conditioning.
