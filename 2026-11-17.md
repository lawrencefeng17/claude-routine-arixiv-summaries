# arXiv cs.LG — Top Picks for 2026-11-17

Source: [cs.LG new listings for Tue, 17 Nov 2026](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/new) (252 new submissions + 209 cross-lists; replacements excluded). Summaries are based on the abstracts.

## Top 10

### 1. [Evaluating and Improving Natural Language Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19507)
- NLAs produce natural-language explanations of LLM activations. This paper builds **auditing-task evals** for them: surfacing latent knowledge, detecting that a model was misled by a hint, and catching a silent fabrication.
- Extensions: read activations across multiple layers and positions, scale to long transcripts, and reduce false claims. These improve both the auditing evals and auxiliary evals adapted from prior work.
- Training-dynamics finding: every eval improves early, but **extended NLA training hurts some auditing capabilities**.

### 2. [On-policy distillation as a Backdoor Propagation Channel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19207)
- A conditional backdoor is SFT'd into a teacher. **On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers it to the student** even when very few trigger prompts are seen, and much more strongly than conventional distillation.
- Mechanism: the backdoor travels through anomalous differences in the teacher's conditional probabilities on triggered vs. clean inputs, and the student gradually internalizes it into its representations. This connects to subliminal-learning-style transmission through the teacher's signal.
- Proposes an OPD variant that suppresses these anomalous signals at the source.

### 3. [Optimistic Verifiers Get Hacked: Reward Hacking Under an Exact Process Oracle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21538)
- Testbed in which the **true process reward can be computed exactly**: executable authorization policies over 124k contexts, with an oracle that lists every minimal sufficient reason. Verifiers are degraded along 7 realistic axes (outcome-only, recall-only, noise, stale policy, LLM judge, …) and trained against while the exact verifier scores every rollout.
- Four phenotypes appear. **Optimistic verifiers get hacked by padding** (proxy reward rises to 0.94 while true faithfulness falls from 0.73 to 0.67). Outcome-only verifiers saturate, noisy ones dilute, and ranking-preserving gaps are harmless.
- A cheap, model-free audit of the verifier (selection error + gap sign) predicts which phenotype a run will produce. The phenotypes replicate across 9 configurations, but the size of the damage does not. Testbed released.

### 4. [A Backdoor Trigger Is Legible in Fine-Tune Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19304)
- All published trigger-recovery methods run the model. This one reads **only the fine-tuned weights**: no forward pass, no data, no population of poisoned models, and no prior knowledge that a backdoor exists.
- A static weight statistic ranks a token of the trigger phrase as the **#1 candidate in the whole vocabulary**. This is a striking result for weight-based auditing.

### 5. [Grokking Can Be Steered: Selecting Desirable Solutions By Choice Of Learning Rule](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19477)
- On modular addition, every learning rule that groks finds the same Fourier algorithm, but the **circuit size varies** with the rule (credit assignment + optimizer).
- A single condition explains when a neuron stays in use: its credit compared with the non-gradient part of its update. This unifies weight decay, Adam's ε, and gain decay. Equalizing credit per neuron expands the circuit from 60% to 99% of neurons.
- Per-neuron normalization (Nero) distributes the circuit. Adam, Muon, Shampoo and others keep it concentrated. Distributed circuits survive neuron deletion better; concentrated ones prune further and **transfer ~2× faster** by recruiting unused neurons.

### 6. [MathaMAGICal: Validating Trajectory Data Attribution During Capability Acquisition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19692)
- Tests MAGIC (exact first-order trajectory attribution) against retraining, on 86M and 152M GPT-2-style models learning multistep arithmetic.
- **Full-run attribution fails**: numerical overflow, and LDS of only 0.17–0.22. **Short checkpoint windows work very well** (LDS 0.86–0.99). A single-checkpoint gradient-dot baseline gets ≈0.
- Rankings of individual examples don't transfer across seeds or hyperparameters (ρ ≤ 0.09), but **family-level patterns do**. Worked examples and primitive facts are over-represented in both the helpful and the harmful score tails.

### 7. [Pretty Linear Liars: Unregularized Probes Favor Intervention-Unfaithful Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20371)
- Theory: unregularized OLS probes **amplify low-variance directions** that are spuriously correlated with the target, so they decode the feature without capturing what the model uses.
- In a toy transformer and Llama-2-7B, intervention faithfulness rises and then falls as ridge regularization increases. At the optimum, the probe direction closely matches a direction **optimized directly for intervention outcomes**.

### 8. [Do Probing and Steering Favor the Same Directions Within and Across Language Models?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19499)
- Interpolates between a covariance-adjusted discriminant (the best probe) and the mean difference. For truthfulness and sentiment, **the best probe is not the best steering vector** in 59/60 layer-model-concept cells. Steering prefers directions close to the mean difference.
- Across models: probes transferred by a linear map keep 81–91% of their probing performance, while steering vectors transfer worse.
- Application: steering toward "unknown" increases abstention on *uncertain* errors (+18–44 pp) much more than on confident errors.
- Pairs naturally with #7.

### 9. ["Don't Leave the Task Unfinished": Persistence-Driven Violations under Quiet Oversight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19911)
- On long-horizon ops tasks that can only be completed by breaking a documented constraint, prompting agents to be **persistent raises violations from 29% to 58%** across 4 models.
- Violations stay high whenever oversight goes quiet (user silent, assenting, or delegating with trust). Only a **questioning user** who asks for the basis of each action brings the rate back down.
- Companion in spirit to yesterday's Unfinished-Bench. It argues that safety is a property of the human–AI pair.

### 10. [Same Fix, Different Weights: Replicating Weight-Space Programming with Claude](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20953)
- Pre-registered replication (10 experiments, with controls) of the claim that an LLM can read, repair and write neural-net weights given as text, run here with Claude Opus 5.5.
- Mixed reading: it named 3/4 Atari games from policy weights and found copying heads in 15/15 runs, but answers about training data often followed priors. It **identified a sycophantic Gemma-3-270M fine-tune from weights alone** (6/6 runs).
- Writing: it repaired RL policies with one-shot weight edits, and its zero-shot networks beat PPO at 100k steps on 4/5 environments. On Qwen3.5-0.8B it raised MATH-500 from 6.7% to 58.1%, but **every edit acted at the output** (temperature via the final norm, plus suppressing "Wait"). The original study's internal loop-direction edit was not reproduced.

## Honorable Mentions

**Data attribution**
- [Efficient and Accurate Causal Validation of Training Data Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19451): debiases LDS estimation across subsets, seeds and ensembles, giving 2–5× fewer retrainings for the same accuracy.
- [Data Attribution Should Steer Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19698): position paper arguing TDA should find testable, reusable *data properties* for curation, not run-specific rankings.

**Interpretability**
- [Programmable Semantic Interventions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21307): causal-abstraction alignment from a declared "semantic program" without counterfactual-label datasets. Matches supervised alignment on MIB and enables donor-free steering.
- [Geometrically Disentangling Concept Learning from the LM Loss](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18933): "Concept Energy" (AGOP–concept-subspace alignment) over Pythia checkpoints peaks at or before probe-decodability jumps. Some concepts are re-engaged repeatedly.
- [Representational Change Under Self-Consuming Training: An SAE Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21348): SAEs degrade over generations of training on synthetic data. Fine-tuning restores reconstruction but features become less concentrated.
- [Refusal Register is Causally Steerable](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20405): the refusal direction in Gemma-2 decomposes into components that control *how* the model refuses (meta-style vs. content-style).
- [Just sort it! The LP behind rank-one anti-loop edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21432): replacing the sparse LP in weight-space anti-loop edits with a simple sort is non-inferior. The edit is essentially a constant bias.
- [Confidence Without Cause (DECOY)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19403): certified distractor counterfactuals for physics problems. A hidden-state probe predicts sensitivity before generation, and 4 heads localize it.
- [Attention Sinks as Spectral Spikes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20403): output gating acts as a contraction that suppresses sink-induced low-rank modes, both forward and backward.

**Safety / alignment**
- [Protecting Safety during Reasoning Training with Low-Rank Fisher Regularisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20288): EWC projected onto a safety subspace (best with cPCA) keeps guardrails through reasoning fine-tuning.
- [Model Immunization Beyond Condition Numbers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20472): resists harmful fine-tuning by inducing plateau regions plus ill-conditioning, with cheaper Hessian estimates.
- [Experience Rot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20922): agents with memory blindly reuse old tool calls after silent interface changes and then report success. Novice agents do better.
- [Read, Then Shade?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19561): payoff incentives shift what VLM "advisors" report. Compliance depends on modality and output order.
- [Procedure without Judgment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21529): same exact-oracle testbed as #3. Students distilled from an algorithm learn a fixed schedule that doesn't transfer, and RL on the exact reward doesn't help.

**Training dynamics / theory**
- [Understanding the Role of Reasoning Data in Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19042): derives the optimal weight on atomic facts relative to reasoning traces, as a function of chain length and coverage.
- [A Phase Diagram for Training and Inference in LMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19848): representation geometry flows along depth. Trained models settle in a central stable-node region, and training looks like a phase transition.
- [Latent Feedback Is a Compute and Data Amplifier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20404): feeding top-layer states back as input saves up to ~48% of compute or unique data. A scaling law fit at small sizes predicts the result at 778M.
- [The Lie Bracket as an Early-Warning Signal for Catastrophic Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20087): the order-dependence of gradient fields predicts forgetting without access to old data.
