## Top 10

### 1. [How Much Does a Reasoning Summary Reveal? An Observability Ladder for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02089)
*Algaba, Carlon, Delcon, Ballon, Verbruggen, Ginis*

- Builds an "observability ladder" holding each model run fixed while varying what's shown to an evaluator: the response alone, a model-generated summary of its reasoning trace, the full trace, or internal signals.
- Without the prompt visible, summaries carry most of the trace's ranking signal (mean AUROC 0.774 vs. 0.813 for the full trace) — but that gain shrinks once the prompt is known.
- The final words of a reasoning trace predict correctness about as well as a full summary, and give better uncertainty estimates.
- Bottom line: in the realistic case where a monitor already knows the prompt, reasoning summaries are a weaker safety-monitoring signal than the raw trace — CoT-faithfulness/oversight relevant.

### 2. [Feed-Forward Steering in Transformer Residual Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02071)
*Mudarisov, Burtsev, State*

- Extends attention-only "particles on a sphere" theories of the residual stream by modeling the FFN as a local steering field acting on each token's direction.
- Splits FFN's effect into a tangential component (steers direction) and a radial component (changes magnitude); a commutator-defect metric flags when attention+FFN can be approximated as parallel, independent flows.
- Validated on GPT-2, Pythia, Mistral, and Llama: ablating the tangential component preserves performance/diversity, while ablating the radial component causes severe degradation.
- Gives a mechanistic, cross-architecture account of what FFN layers are actually doing to the residual stream — useful for steering-vector and activation-patching work.

### 3. [GradCuit: Credit-Assigned Gradient Flow Enables Robust and Interpretable Test-Time Latent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02585)
*Yu, Shen, Li, Zhang, Zhu, Zhang, Zheng*

- Test-time latent reasoning method: inserts learnable latent states inside a transformer layer and uses causal self-attention to build a differentiable path from later tokens back to those latents, so reward-weighted gradients directly shape reasoning — no weight updates.
- Beats chain-of-thought prompting by 6.6 points (64.5% avg. accuracy) and is more robust to learning-rate choice than competing latent-reasoning methods.
- Token-level gradient attribution shows early-to-middle layers do most of the useful optimization — an interpretability byproduct of the training-time credit-assignment mechanism itself.

### 4. [One QK Channel, Many Sources: Guarding Low-Precision Attention Collapse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02091)
*Xie, Xie, Cao, Ran, Yang, Xie*

- Investigates catastrophic bf16 training collapse in transformers and finds that although errors originate from different low-precision sources, they all funnel through one shared bottleneck: the query-key (QK) attention channel.
- Shows the QK projection's largest singular value stays stable only when attention logits are normalized, i.e., the failure is really an unnormalized-logit-saturation problem.
- Proposes QK-Guard: a dormant, parameter-free QK normalizer that switches on only when logit saturation begins, matching always-on normalization's stability at lower cost.
- A concrete training-dynamics/numerics finding with direct implications for why large training runs silently destabilize.

### 5. [Measuring in-context algorithmic reasoning in language models against an exact Bayes-optimal standard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01575)
*(F-ICL benchmark)*

- Introduces F-ICL, a benchmark where ground truth is exactly computable: 1.5 billion programs enumerated, with the true Bayes-optimal posterior derived in closed form under a bounded universal prior.
- Despite up to 92% raw accuracy, 45 of 46 evaluated models produce posteriors farther from Bayes-optimal than a trivial keystroke-count baseline.
- Finds non-monotone updating (more examples can *hurt*), a scale-invariant gap that frontier models don't close, and a gap that *widens* after instruction/reasoning post-training.
- Direct evidence that LLM "in-context reasoning" leans on low-order statistical shortcuts rather than genuine algorithmic inference — squarely in science-of-generalization territory.

### 6. [On the Identifiability of Masked Prediction: Mode Blindness and Mask Schedules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01383)

- Theoretical study of when masked-prediction training can actually recover the true data distribution, using a two-mode toy setting.
- Proves that mask schedules dominated by large visible contexts are provably blind to global mode weights, even at near-zero excess risk — quantified via a new "ε-identifiability modulus."
- Low-visibility masks (or masks with nonzero probability of full masking) restore identifiability of the joint distribution without further assumptions.
- Validated via exact enumeration, gradient-based training, and measurements on real text — places natural-language masked LMs within this identifiability landscape, relevant to why MLM/BERT-style objectives learn what they learn.

### 7. [The Fourth Quadrant: A Stylized View of Benign Misfitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01032)

- Studies a linear-regression regime the authors call "benign misfitting": predictors that generalize well are provably forced to have *worse* training error than the zero predictor.
- Characterizes two sample thresholds (span-predictor generalization vs. interpolation) and shows one-pass large-learning-rate SGD achieves near-optimal test error throughout the gap between them, despite large training error.
- Connects the same "nuisance" coordinate driving training misfit to the predictor's adversarial robustness — a generalization-theory extension of the benign-overfitting literature with a robustness angle.

### 8. [Z-PEFT: Zero-shot Backdoor Detection in Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning via Canonical Spectral Signatures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02271)
*Pitzalis, Shenaj, Cignoni, Cossu, Bacciu, Carta*

- Targets the real-world threat of downloading backdoored PEFT adapters from open repositories; studies weight-space backdoor detection (classify malicious vs. clean from weights alone).
- Explicitly evaluates the harder, more realistic *zero-shot* setting — unseen attack types and datasets — instead of the usual closed-world train/test-on-same-attack setup.
- Z-PEFT, a lightweight meta-classifier using only layer-wise spectral measures, gets the best zero-shot accuracy among weight-space detectors, at low computational cost.
- Shows that strong closed-world detectors often fail to generalize, a useful caution for supply-chain AI-safety tooling.

### 9. [Secrets Everywhere: Auditing Memorization in Mobility Prediction Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02052)
*Kouam, Boyadzhiev, Rieck*

- Extends LLM-style memorization auditing to mobility/location-trajectory prediction models, where privacy risk operates at multiple scales (single locations vs. multi-step trajectory segments).
- Builds reference datasets to test whether models systematically prefer real training trajectories over equally plausible synthetic alternatives.
- Finds pervasive memorization that correlates with how "regular" a user's mobility pattern is, increasing extraction risk at inference time.
- Argues privacy/memorization audits should be mandatory before deployment — directly relevant to data-attribution and training-data-leakage questions outside the usual LM context.

### 10. [Toward Plasticity-Preserving KL Regularization for Capability Retention in LLM Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01743)

- Addresses capability loss during LLM RL fine-tuning: standard KL regularization constrains the *entire* output distribution against a reference policy, which over-anchors even incorrect behaviors and limits exploration.
- Proposes Correctness-Conditioned KL (CoKL): restricts the preservation constraint to correctness-conditioned response distributions, protecting known-good behavior while leaving room to explore new solutions.
- Shows standard KL regularization creates an unavoidable "correctness gap" whenever the reference policy is imperfect, while CoKL avoids it by construction.
- A concrete alignment/RLHF training-dynamics contribution to the plasticity-vs.-capability-retention tradeoff during post-training.
