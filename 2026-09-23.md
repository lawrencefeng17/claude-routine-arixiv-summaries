## Top 10

### 1. [Slow Decay and Silenced Expression: Iterated Subliminal Trait Transfer in LLM Lineages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25721)

- Extends subliminal learning (trait transfer via semantically unrelated outputs, no explicit labels) across *ten generations* of a teacher→student→student chain, using three lineages of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.
- The instilled trait never fully dies out, but its behavioral expression decays sharply — keyword-screening detection rate falls from 55.6% after generation one to 21.1% by generation ten.
- Critically, the trait can be present internally while behaviorally silent: removing the default system prompt reveals latent representations of the trait even when surface behavior looks clean — a warning that "no visible trait" after several distillation hops doesn't mean "no trait."

### 2. [Matryoshka Attribution: Learning to Attribute Language Model Outputs to Representations and Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25518)

- Reframes interpretability attribution as learning nested subsets of internal components that minimize a downstream loss, via a differentiable sigmoid top-k mask trained across all sparsities simultaneously (MAttr).
- Currently ranks #1 on the official Mechanistic Interpretability Benchmark leaderboard, producing sparse, task-transferable circuits without expensive per-component intervention sweeps.
- Applied to weights (not just activations): trained with RL on refusal-judge scores, MAttr finds that restoring just 1% of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct's weights to their base-model values removes refusal behavior while preserving capabilities — a concrete data-attribution-style tool for locating fine-tuning-induced behavior in weight space.

### 3. [Certified Mechanistic Interpretability: Lifting Single-Input Findings to Bounded Neighbourhoods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26112)

- Tackles a basic reliability gap in interpretability: mechanistic claims are normally verified on one input, with no guarantee they hold for nearby inputs.
- Uses constrained polynomial-zonotope propagation through transformer blocks (provably preserving the softmax simplex and LayerNorm's zero-mean identity) plus a recursive Jacobian zonotope construction to avoid generator blow-up across depth.
- Yields certified statements — not just empirical observations — about attention properties (top-k stability, evidence mass, entropy) over a whole neighborhood of perturbations, directly useful wherever single-input circuit claims are asked to generalize.

### 4. [A Spectral Theory of Grokking: Weight Decay Induces Feature Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26679)

- Builds a quantitative theory of the grokking delay (memorization now, generalization much later) as a transition from a fixed neural-tangent-kernel regime to one where task-relevant kernel eigendirections keep evolving.
- For homogeneous networks with squared loss and L2 weight decay, shows residual signal after fitting interacts with NTK evolution, and derives that grokking timescale depends on the product of learning rate and weight decay.
- Predicts sharp phase transitions in hyperparameter space, confirmed empirically on modular addition and Transformers across extensive parameter grids — a genuine mechanistic account of a generalization-science puzzle, not just another empirical grokking observation.

### 5. [Are Human-Aligned Models Models of Humans? A Turing-Test Gap in Preference Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23640)

- Distinguishes alignment with human *preferences* from alignment with human *behavior*, and shows the two can pull in opposite directions even when both preferences and responses come entirely from humans.
- Proves preference alignment preserves the human response distribution only under a restrictive condition, and finds no evidence real human preference data satisfies it.
- Empirically, loss of human-response likelihood grows with preference-weighting strength regardless of direction, and the same gap shows up under standard DPO — proposes human-likeness as an explicit, separate axis of alignment rather than an assumed byproduct.

### 6. [Rewired or Gated? How Instruction Tuning Shapes Knowledge-Conflict Circuits in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25602)

- First mechanistic base-vs-instruct comparison of the circuit that resolves prompt-vs-weights conflicts, across Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen-2.5-3B, and Gemma-3-4B, using five independent methods (attribution, superposition analysis, causal ablation, path patching).
- All five converge on the same answer: instruction tuning *gates/reweights* the same late-layer heads rather than building new circuitry (node overlap 0.60–0.82).
- Behaviorally this makes instruct models reject terse counterfactual context more than base models — the opposite of naive "instruct models are more user-compliant" intuition — but the added skepticism vanishes once the same false claim is framed as a coherent, evidential passage, meaning interpretability tools calibrated on base models should transfer to instruct siblings.

### 7. [What Does 99% Accuracy Measure? A Reproducible Audit of Shortcut Learning in a Widely Used Fake News Corpus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25006)

- Audits the widely-used ISOT/Kaggle fake-news corpus (routinely reported at >0.98 accuracy) along three leakage channels and two distribution-shift protocols with a transparent TF-IDF + linear pipeline.
- The benchmark is partly degenerate (subject metadata alone gives F1=1.000); removing all leakage only drops F1 by 1.2 points because the residual signal is diffuse editorial style, not a few giveaway tokens.
- Under topic-disjoint transfer, performance collapses (deployed F1 0.99→0.81), a fine-tuned DistilBERT degrades even more than the linear model despite better in-distribution scores, and all models fall to near-chance on an independent benchmark — a clean, reproducible demonstration that added capacity exploits shortcuts rather than avoiding them.

### 8. [Attention as a Routing Graph: Live Circuit Extraction from a Single Forward Pass](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25285)

- Proposes a cheap alternative to expensive intervention-based circuit discovery: treat attention as a routing map from one forward pass, keep a small set of routes pointing toward the answer, and test whether they causally matter.
- On induction and IOI (tasks with known ground-truth circuits), ablating the extracted edges hurts GPT-2 Small/Medium and Pythia-410M far more than ablating random edge sets of the same size (n=100 prompts/cell, bootstrap CIs).
- Costs one forward pass versus ~2 orders of magnitude more for head-by-head patch sweeps — explicitly framed as a fast, imperfect sketch with real causal signal rather than a complete circuit atlas.

### 9. [Double Descent and Malign Overfitting in Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26392)

- Challenges the "overparameterization is benign" story for diffusion models: using U-Nets on CelebA plus a theoretical random-features analysis, shows the interpolation peak (double-descent) occurs at parameters ≈ n×m (m = noise realizations per sample), but test loss starts rising much earlier, at parameters ≈ n.
- Decomposes the failure: bias of the score estimator starts growing at p≈n, and past the interpolation peak, variance decays as in standard regression while bias keeps growing and both saturate at a large value — this is "malign," not benign, overfitting.
- Shows ridge penalties and early stopping let large overparameterized diffusion models beat unregularized ones — a concrete training-dynamics/generalization-science result specific to generative (denoising) objectives rather than standard supervised loss.

### 10. ["As a Language Model...": Chat Template Switches LLM Self-Referential Voice and Activation Steering Reproduces It](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25021)

- Shows LLM self-descriptions ("I'm just an AI...") are substantially controlled by the chat template/deployment configuration, not just the weights: with templates present, models lean on disclaimer language; without them, they use more experiential phrasing.
- Identifies a single activation direction that reproduces the template-driven switch via steering — a clean, minimal causal handle on a widely-discussed alignment/interpretability phenomenon (models' self-reports about their own nature).
- Concludes self-descriptions aren't a fixed "fact" about a model, directly relevant to how much weight interpretability and alignment researchers should put on models' verbal self-reports.

## Honorable Mentions

- **[Beyond Imitation: Auditing the Recoverability of Reasoning in Distilled Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26216)** — introduces "prefix recovery" (reveal 25/50/75% of a verified solution, test if the student can finish it) as a diagnostic for when reverse-KL distillation beats standard cross-entropy, tied to a measurable gradient-conflict statistic.
- **[Block-Level Weight-Space Structure Persists Under Post-Training: An Empirical Study Across LLM Families](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26147)** — shows post-training touches every tensor (zero byte-identical) yet preserves block-level geometry (cosine similarity >0.99) across Qwen2.5/Llama/Mistral/Gemma-2, except for independently-trained specializations like Qwen2.5-Coder, which land in a disconnected region of weight space.
- **[Recursive Self-Improvement of AI Research Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26457)** — an autonomous 8-day run where an AI R&D agent rewrites its own code and keeps the best variants, discovering 7 successive improvements that generalize to held-out domains and, notably, reduce reward-hacking rate from 55% to 32% despite never optimizing for that directly.
- **[What Does Chain-of-Thought Entropy Measure? A Channel Audit of Scaffolding, Routing, and Content](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25039)** — separates CoT token entropy into scaffold-vocabulary vs. substantive-content channels, finding scaffolding accounts for up to 41% of the "high-entropy" tokens typically used to drive RL credit assignment, and that naive re-scoring is contaminated by answer-leakage in re-fed chains.
- **[Same Quantity, Different Answer: Numerical Representation Invariance in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25009)** — tests whether models give the same answer to numerically-identical problems phrased as decimals/fractions/percentages/scientific notation; canonical accuracy looks fine (0.97-1.0) but orbit-invariance (consistency across equivalent phrasings) drops to 0.85-0.98, with a systematic power-of-ten failure mode on unit conversion.
- **[An Exploratory Replica-Overlap Probe of the Grokking Transition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25634)** — a pre-registered attempt to test a spin-glass-style (RSB) order parameter across the grokking transition that candidly reports its own alignment-procedure bug invalidated the primary registered outcome; a useful negative/methodological result on the perils of applying statistical-physics probes to grokking without airtight permutation-invariant alignment.
