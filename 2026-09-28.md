## Top 10

### 1. [User Model Extraction via Belief Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31603)

- Introduces Belief Self-Distillation (BSD): the frozen LLM acts as its own teacher, distilling a compact, decodable *and* causally-writable representation of its inferred beliefs about the user, purely from natural conversations with no external annotation.
- Key finding: refusal depends not just on the request but on the model's inferred user identity — rewriting the belief state changes refusal behavior while holding the request text fixed.
- Independently trained LLMs converge on a *shared geometry* for representing their users — a cross-model regularity with direct implications for how models condition safety decisions on assumed user identity.

### 2. [Which Influence Are We Estimating? The Role of Counterfactual Specifications in Data Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31214)

- Argues that disagreement between influence/data-attribution estimators is often not approximation error but **specification mismatch**: different implicit choices of target behavior, intervention, and counterfactual training process.
- Formalizes influence as a counterfactual estimand and derives a local decomposition separating behavior signals, training signals, and counterfactual parameter response.
- Shows exact estimands under different specifications yield genuinely different rankings; behavior-aligned specifications recover target-specific training examples that default loss- or similarity-based attribution methods miss.

### 3. [Guarded Gradient-Based Activation Steering of Shutdown Responses in Qwen3.5-0.8B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30326)

- Direct corrigibility study: derives steering directions from gradients of logit differences (rather than paired-activation contrasts) to shift shutdown-avoidance responses (KEEP) toward acceptance (STOP), gated by a classifier that decides *when* to intervene.
- Achieves 75% recall / 90% precision on detecting shutdown-relevant scenarios, with false-positive interventions causing no unwanted decision changes.
- Effect is real but narrow and selective — steering converts avoidance to acceptance only when Qwen itself is the one being shut down, not for shutdown of other processes.

### 4. [Cosine Similarity Is Not Evidence: Measuring the Noise Floor of Interpretability Transfer Under Quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30275)

- Methodological critique in the vein of recent "alignment illusion" work: interpretability artifacts (steering directions, probes) calibrated on full-precision weights are certified as surviving quantization using scale-invariant statistics (cosine similarity, AUROC) reported without their noise floor.
- Derives the noise floor for difference-in-means direction estimators as a function of class separation and sample size/dimension (κ = nρ²/d), showing published similarity numbers can't distinguish genuine preservation from random agreement.
- Empirically on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct: INT4 quantization rotates directions beyond the noise margin while INT8 shows no detectable movement — and scale-invariant statistics can't tell translation apart from attenuation of a decision variable.

### 5. [LocUS: Head Selection and Subspace Projection for Targeted Activation Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31122)

- Addresses a core weakness of activation steering: per-layer directions estimated from contrastive data get coupled to off-target properties, degrading unrelated capabilities.
- Grounds steering to the model's own output-vocabulary subspace, identifying a property-specific linear subspace in the unembedding matrix and localizing intervention to a sparse subset of attention heads.
- Matches or beats state-of-the-art baselines on toxicity mitigation, sentiment redirection, and sycophancy suppression while touching under 6% of parameters and better preserving general capability.

### 6. [Adaptive Multi-Value Control in LLMs via Causal Activation Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30405)

- Tackles multi-value alignment: prior steering work treats human/moral values in isolation, while naive composition uses fixed intervention strengths that can't adapt to the model's evolving internal state.
- AIMES builds layer-specific bipolar directions per moral-foundation value plus intermediate-layer vocabulary readouts as online "observers," then adapts each value's steering strength per decoding step without training a separate value-state estimator.
- Shows depth-dependent gains over fixed-joint and prompt-based steering with smaller realized interventions and comparable response quality — evidence for lightweight, state-aware alignment at inference time.

### 7. [The Linear Representation Hypothesis for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30996)

- Extends the linear representation hypothesis (standard interpretability lens for LLMs) to embodied VLA models, where a quantity of interest co-evolves with system dynamics rather than being a static semantic attribute.
- Develops a signature-based theory unifying representations and policies, proving representations exist from which a physical quantity's future evolution under a candidate action trajectory is linearly probeable.
- Derives a signature generalized linear model for stochastic action chunks giving monotonic change in expected future outcomes along linear parameter paths — enabling linear steering of embodied policies, verified in a planar control-affine navigation experiment.

### 8. [The Residual Stream's Effective Depth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31098)

- Introduces "effective depth" (D_eff), a scalar diagnostic treating a transformer's residual stream as a discrete-time process and measuring how representation similarity decays with layer distance.
- Across 16 decoder-only LMs, 15 of 16 fall well below the theoretical maximum for maximally diverse orthogonal layer updates (e.g., Qwen3.5 at 32–44%, Pythia at 23–28%) — driven by correlated residual updates, not literally unused depth (ruled out via controls).
- Tracked across training, OLMo-2 settles into this low-effective-depth regime early and holds it through 5T tokens, while Pythia-1.4B trends downward — a new lens on training dynamics and architectural depth utilization.

### 9. [Auditing System-1 Models on Biosecurity-Relevant Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30454)

- AI-safety auditing of cheap, non-autoregressive "System-1" classifiers (increasingly used as inexpensive pipeline components) on biosecurity-relevant benchmarks: WMDP, a paraphrase-robust WMDP-Bio variant, and LAB-Bench subtasks (6,020 items total).
- Once the vendor's uncertainty field is correctly interpreted, the audited model is reasonably calibrated (ECE 0.034, AUROC 0.820) but degrades substantially on harder tasks.
- Finds a concerning robustness gap: cyclically rotating multiple-choice answer order flips 37.4% of WMDP-Cyber answers (not run-to-run noise); averaging over rotations recovers most of the accuracy loss cheaply — a practical reliability finding for anyone deploying such models in safety-relevant screening.

### 10. [In-Context Binding Capacity in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30634)

- Science-of-generalization study measuring how many variable-to-value assignments a language model can track in-context before recall collapses, via continuous recall curves across 12 models ≤3B plus 30 additional models up to 12B.
- The load at which recall falls halfway to chance scales as K₅₀ = cN^α with α ≈ 0.82 (R² = 0.73); pretraining choices alone produce an eightfold range in measured capacity.
- Direct task-specific training improves scores, but differing measurement criteria mean this can't be read as a genuine capacity gain — ties in-context binding to working-memory and instruction-following limits while carefully scoping what the metric does and doesn't capture (no measurement of state updates or downstream transfer).

## Honorable Mentions

- **[Estimating and Orthogonalizing Unknown Pre-training Gradients for Continual Fine-tuning of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30935)** — tackles catastrophic forgetting of general-purpose pretraining knowledge during continual fine-tuning by estimating pre-training gradients via learnable pseudo-data (Gumbel-Softmax soft prompt) and enforcing orthogonality against new-task updates.
- **[A Flatness-Generalization Relation in the Teacher-Student Tree-Committee Machine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31101)** — analytically tractable teacher-student model testing the popular "flatness implies generalization" heuristic; finds the correlation holds only in specific parameter/data regimes and can even reverse for underparameterized classifiers.
- **[I Act Therefore I Am: When Is JEPA's Action-Conditioning Enough to Learn Causal Mechanisms?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31161)** — identifiability theory for joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPA/world models), proving conditions (sufficient action-induced variation) under which learned representations recover true latent causal states up to invertible transformation.
- **[New LoRA Skills Should Read but Never Write](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31600)** — training-dynamics fix for LoRA adapter composition: canonicalizes adapter factorizations and constrains new-skill coupling to be one-directional (read old skills, never overwrite them), avoiding interference when appending skills sequentially.
- **[Decodable In-Context State and Model Output Across Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31401)** — tracks probe-decodable in-context variable bindings and probe-guided steering across Pythia pretraining checkpoints, with an information-theoretic argument for why error-only decodability can't establish discarded output information.
- **[Accounting for Bias Enables Sustainable LLM Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31184)** — proposes a latent-variable measurement model that explicitly corrects LLM-as-judge evaluation for position/verbosity/severity biases, recovering reliable rankings from far fewer comparisons than current leaderboard practice.
- **[Learning Chance-Constrained MDPs with Bellman Distributional Certificates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30856)** — safe-RL theory showing that probability-level (chance) safety constraints, despite being nonconvex and trajectory-dependent, don't necessarily carry a higher statistical price than expectation-based safety, via a reusable "Bellman distributional certificate."
