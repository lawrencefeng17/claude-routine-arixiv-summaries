## Top 10

### 1. [Subliminal Learning as Trait-Direction Drift: A Mechanism and Targeted Control under SFT Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01091)

- Proposes and validates a mechanism for subliminal learning — where distilling on a teacher's semantically clean data (e.g. plain numeric sequences) still transfers the teacher's hidden trait to the student: biased generation leaves a measurable preference gap in the teacher's data, and student-recognizable gaps drive trait-aligned parameter updates during SFT that accumulate into behavioral transfer.
- Introduces "probe-space corridor regularization," a targeted defense that constrains drift along a calibrated trait direction during distillation, cutting malicious-response transfer from 29.55% to 6.45% at low cost to main-task accuracy.
- Directly demystifies one of the year's most-discussed emergent alignment phenomena by tying it to preference-gap, training-trajectory, and causal-intervention evidence rather than treating it as a black-box curiosity.

### 2. [Pre-carved Niches: The Formation Dynamics of Modular Task Partitions in Early LLM Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01170)

- Tracks, step by step, how the modular internal organization seen in finished LLMs actually forms — training a Pythia-410M model from scratch and running attribution patching plus gradient/weight-norm probes across 14 tasks in four cognitive domains at every step.
- Finds the modular map is "pre-carved": before any learning occurs, the eventual dominant task-pairing already overlaps at ~3.6x a task-independent baseline, and the partition then locks in through two sharp, LR-schedule-independent jumps accompanied by gradient-level "relative deprivation" between winning and losing circuits.
- A rare longitudinal (not post-hoc) study of training dynamics and interpretability together, with a pre-registered scale-threshold hypothesis for an ongoing 2.8B follow-up.

### 3. [Context-Grounding Gains Are Mediated by Pre-existing Machinery: Auditing GRPO, SFT, and DPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00925)

- Asks whether post-training methods that make LLMs follow contextual evidence over memorized knowledge build new machinery or just strengthen machinery already present, comparing nine post-training arms (five GRPO variants, SFT, DPO) from one starting checkpoint against a pre-training-estimated "grounding direction."
- Finds GRPO's grounding gains are small and largely explained by existing causal attention-head circuitry; DPO drives grounding near ceiling but via the same starting-model machinery, and adding the starting model's own direction recovers 35% of DPO's gain.
- A clean mechanistic audit of exactly which alignment techniques teach new behavior versus merely amplify latent capability — directly relevant to interpreting what RLHF/DPO pipelines are actually doing.

### 4. [Beyond Scores: Understanding LLM-as-a-Judge Mechanisms in Summarization Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01604)

- Mechanistically reverse-engineers how LLM judges (Themis/Llama-3-8B, Prometheus/Mistral-7B) assign quality ratings, using causal tracing, logit-lens projection, and attention-head knockout on a controlled clean/corrupt summary-pair pipeline.
- Finds a consistent two-stage circuit: below layer 15, attention performs local error comparison and routes it to the final token; above it, an MLP cascade integrates the signal and crystallizes the rating at a sharp late layer (L≈25–26).
- A base-model control shows fine-tuning installs the stage separation onto a pre-existing routing substrate rather than building the judge pipeline from scratch — important for anyone treating LLM-as-judge scores as trustworthy training or evaluation signals.

### 5. [Confess What You Know: Forget-Set Misalignment with Model Knowledge in LLM Unlearning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00605)

- Identifies "forget-set misalignment" in machine unlearning: when the pre-defined forget set doesn't match what a model actually memorized (common when original training data is inaccessible for privacy reasons), unlearning either leaves leakage (Under Unlearning) or needlessly damages unrelated knowledge (Out-of-Knowledge Unlearning).
- Uses gradient-level analysis to show this is a targeting problem, not an optimizer problem, then proposes CONFS, a data-blind method that elicits the model's own memorized knowledge to construct a model-aligned forget set.
- Approaches gold-standard forgetting-utility tradeoffs across synthetic, multimodal, and real-world benchmarks — a practically important fix for a failure mode that undermines real-world unlearning deployments.

### 6. [How Do Language Models Choose Between Context and Memory?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00753)

- Studies the causal status of "authority directions" — activation directions that decode whether a model will trust supplied context versus its own parametric knowledge when they conflict — via counterfactual coordinate-interchange experiments rather than steering alone.
- Across Qwen, Llama, and OLMo, interchanging naturally occurring coordinates along authority directions reproduces 30–68% of the authority-induced behavioral shift, while matched controls reproduce almost none, establishing genuine (not merely correlational) causal use.
- Cross-task transfer of these directions is weak (closes only 9% of the gap vs. 57% for a same-task direction), suggesting knowledge-source arbitration is computed in a task-dependent rather than universally reusable way — a nuanced result for interpretability work that assumes single general-purpose "truth" or "authority" directions.

### 7. [Patterning in Practice: Debiasing Reward Models with Susceptibilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00699)

- Applies "patterning" — reweighting preference pairs by their measured effect on posterior benchmark-loss expectations (their singular-learning-theory "susceptibility") — to debias a Gemma 2 9B reward model against length/formatting biases, the first application of the technique beyond small/synthetic settings.
- Gains +14.2pp on RM-Bench Hard (where style cues point against correctness) while preserving overall accuracy, competitive with the strongest published comparator; traces a side-effect regression on a safety subset back to a small class of training pairs via ablation.
- Notably, the computed weights transfer without recomputation to debias Gemma 2 2B and 27B and partially transfer to Llama 3.1 8B — a rare demonstration of theoretically grounded, transferable RLHF data reweighting.

### 8. [Attention Sensitivity Is Not Enough: Dissociating Attention-Level and Behavioural In-Context Learning under Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00064)

- Tests whether attention-based proxies for in-context learning (ICL) preservation are trustworthy training targets, formalizing "In-Context Sensitivity" (attention distance under demonstration swaps) versus the actual behavioral accuracy gap (ICL-GAP).
- On Llama-2-7B, a regularizer that maximizes the attention proxy drives it to near its ceiling while behavioral ICL-GAP stays near zero and MMLU accuracy drops 0.371→0.279 — a clean Goodhart's-law dissociation where the attention sharpens onto formatting tokens rather than labels.
- A useful methodological caution for the growing body of interpretability-guided training interventions: an internal diagnostic can be fully "satisfied" while the capability it's meant to track silently degrades.

### 9. [Lingua Franca or Probing Artifact? Rethinking Latent Language in Multilingual LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00155)

- Interrogates the popular claim that multilingual LLMs route computation through a language-specific "pivot" (often English) by comparing representation-geometry probes (GMM on hidden states) against decoding-based probes across model families, training regimes, tasks, checkpoints, and 27 languages.
- Finds the two probe families systematically disagree: geometry-based probes detect earlier cross-lingual mixing while decoding-based probes retain sharper, more English-biased signals — differences that track multilinguality and training progression but stay stable across domains.
- A methodologically important result for interpretability broadly: it shows widely used probing techniques can measure genuinely different underlying phenomena rather than converging on one ground truth, undercutting confident claims about a single internal "lingua franca."

### 10. [EvoFlint: An Evolutionary Atlas of Multi-Turn LLM Vulnerabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00487)

- Reframes multi-turn jailbreak red-teaming as a search problem rather than a generation problem: evolves a diverse, organized archive of phased attack strategies (not raw prompts) via LLM-driven mutation/crossover, quality-diversity selection, and cross-generation memory of what worked against a target.
- Reaches attack success rates of 35.8% on Claude Sonnet 4.6, 59.7% on GPT-5.4, and 94.3% on Qwen3-32B on HarmBench-test — producing, for each target, a risk-category map of which harms its safety training has and hasn't covered.
- A substantial advance in systematic, coverage-oriented red-teaming methodology over one-off jailbreak discovery, directly useful for auditing frontier-model safety training.

## Honorable Mentions

- **[Scaled Idempotence in Transformer Attention: Paired OV Geometry and Shared-Value Algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01129)** — finds a sparse subset of attention heads across 6 pretrained models (2.8B–235B) nearly satisfy $T^2 \approx \alpha T$ under their OV operator, with trained key orientation (not random capacity) driving the effect in 98.64% of heads — a striking algebraic regularity in trained attention geometry.
- **[Spawn Freely, Act Sparingly: Progressive Risk Vesting for Recursive LLM-Agent Trees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01035)** — a theoretical safety framework for recursive/multi-agent LLM systems that holds a trajectory-level risk budget in escrow and proves an anytime harm bound, with a phase-transition result (harm scales as √p at a critical "authority reproduction number") governing when granting agents autonomous action authority becomes dangerous.
- **[Investigating Linear Probe Robustness to Linguistic Register, Medical Specialty, and Corpus Shifts in Medical QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01361)** — isolates writing-style, domain, and dataset shifts in truth-direction probe transfer, finding the "truth direction" is robust to register and specialty but breaks unevenly across corpora, showing linear probes partly track dataset structure rather than pure factual content.
- **[Post-Training Science for Supervised Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01244)** — a large controlled sweep across Qwen3/Llama, dense/MoE up to 235B, and real customer SFT datasets measuring how learning rate, batch size, LoRA-vs-full-FT, and epochs actually transfer across scale and family, with uncertainty attached to each recommendation.
- **[The Multiple Timescales of Gradient Descent on the Edge of Stability: A Perturbative Derivation of the Central Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01034)** — gives a formal, multiple-timescales derivation of the "central flow" model of edge-of-stability training dynamics, separating a fast oscillation timescale, an intermediate self-stabilization timescale, and the slow central-flow timescale that previously lacked rigorous justification.
- **[CopyShield: A Cross-Level Benchmark of Copyright Defenses in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01161)** — compares copyright defenses at output (contrastive decoding), behavioral (DPO), and representation (activation intervention) levels under one protocol, finding each level trades off literal-leakage suppression against degeneracy/utility differently — useful evidence on where in the stack alignment interventions should live.
- **[Exploring Sparse Autoencoders in Text-Based Causal Confounding Adjustment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01322)** — uses SAE features, selected via iterative conditional-independence testing, as text representations for causal confounding adjustment, getting lower bias and better coverage than alternative representations while gaining interpretability-driven falsifiability.
- **[Topological Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00597)** — proposes steering LLM behavior via persistence diagrams over activation-space topology rather than raw activation/feature directions, aiming for robustness to outliers and distributional shift that standard activation steering lacks.
- **[Capability-Gated Language Models: Security Composes, Utility Does Not](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00445)** — formalizes per-principal access control inside one set of model weights as a lattice, proving security composes at intersections of restrictions under a falsifiable monotone-elicitation assumption, while showing individually-harmless capability profiles can jointly damage model utility with no compositional bound.
