# arXiv cs.LG Digest — 2026-10-26

Top 10 papers from today's cs.LG listing (Mon 26 Oct 2026, 198 entries) most related to ML, LLMs, AI safety, alignment, generalization, interpretability, data attribution, and training dynamics. About two thirds of the listing's titles were scanned (the page truncates); top picks were read at the abstract level and condensed below. "Questioning Instrumental Convergence in RLVR" (2610.31624) also appears in this listing but was already covered in the 2026-10-23 digest, so it is omitted.

### 1. [Interpretable sparse fine-tuning with TopKLoRA enables faithful circuit discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32866)

*Marek Masiak, Andrzej Szablewski, Nicola Cancedda, Constantin Venhoff*

- TopKLoRA: a LoRA variant where only the top-k adapter latents fire per token, trained purely on downstream loss (unlike SAEs), so latents are causally relevant.
- Sleeper-agent backdoor study on gemma-2-2b and Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B: finds and ablates circuits necessary and sufficient for the backdoor.
- Circuits are up to 10x smaller than from dense LoRA; ablation removes the malicious behavior while preserving instruction-following.

### 2. [Learning to Be Monitored: Causal Bounds and Intervention-Aware Post-Training Recipes for Chain-of-Thought Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31733)

*Qiang Fu, Xinyue Li, Andre Wibisono, Arman Cohan*

- Argues the common minimal-criterion TPR metric overstates how often CoT monitors catch behavior an intervention actually caused.
- Shows it is an upper bound on causal detection rate and derives a complementary lower bound.
- Trains models with off-policy self-distillation, RL, and on-policy self-distillation; all generally make CoTs easier to monitor than base models.

### 3. [REACT: Representational Early Analysis and Control of Chain-of-Thought Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32113)

*Hanwen Zhang, Enyi Jiang, Ruike Zhu, Qinan Yu, Sanmi Koyejo*

- Learns a safety direction from paired safe/unsafe reasoning trajectories; applies one activation edit to an early CoT hidden state (weights frozen).
- Gains concentrate in a 2–3 token early window; effect vanishes by tokens 4–5 even though later states predict risk better.
- On DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B (StrongREJECT-273), fully safe outputs rise from 29.1% to 82.1% with benign usefulness near baseline.

### 4. [How Much Do You Trust A Dog? Quantifying Hint-Following in Reasoning Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32055)

*Anxin Guo, Chirag Pabbaraju, Sara Ahmadian, Kamesh Munagala, Aravindan Vijayaraghavan*

- Tests whether MMLU answers shift when a hint is attributed to a professor, friend, or dog (Qwen, Gemma, SmolLM).
- Hint-driven changes follow an exponential-tilt pattern: a roughly constant log-odds shift regardless of source.
- Formal decomposition shows how KL-regularized reward training reshapes hinted/unhinted distributions.
- Shift persists even when the source is flagged untrustworthy, implying vulnerability to adversarial steering.

### 5. [Principled Nonlinear Concept Erasure and Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32516)

*Idan Simai, Shauli Ravfogel, Uri Shaham*

- Conditional-autoencoder framework for nonlinear concept erasure; perfect reconstruction plus population-level independence yields nonlinear guardedness under stated assumptions.
- Better utility–erasure trade-off than baselines (even supervised ones) without target-task labels; converges faster.
- Also supports concept conversion/steering by changing the target concept at inference.

### 6. [TRACE: Data-Free Text Reconstruction Attacks against Approximate Unlearning in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31995)

*Mengying Zhang, Derui Wang, Ibrahim Khalil, Bowen Liu, Xiaoyu Xia, Minhui Xue*

- Reconstructs "unlearned" training text using only pre- and post-unlearning model snapshots, with no prior knowledge of the data.
- Narrows candidates via token-level embedding parameter differences, then builds sequences by scoring perplexity gaps between the two models.
- High-fidelity recovery on TOFU, MUSE, WMDP across six unlearning methods; argues for stricter snapshot access controls.

### 7. [OP-Print: Tracing Membership Imprints in On-Policy Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31616)

*Qi Li, Xinchao Wang*

- First study of membership inference against on-policy self-distillation.
- Training signal is sparse and concentrated at uncertainty-sensitive points; pairing two views of an example may raise memorization.
- OP-Print (gray-box: teacher–student residual aggregation; black-box: output stability across restarts) works where baselines are near chance.

### 8. [DRIVE: Fine-tuning via Data Contribution- and Diversity-aware Weighting with Prior Regularization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32501)

*Qing Liu, Xinrui Chen, Yi Du, Ou Wu, Weiyao Zhu*

- Targets catastrophic forgetting in fine-tuning when pretraining data is unavailable.
- Per-sample weights from one differentiable objective; Shapley-style additivity splits each sample's contribution into anti-forgetting and adaptation parts, with a covariance diversity term and a prior.
- Claims stability–plasticity guarantees and better performance/convergence; code released.

### 9. [On-Policy Self-Training: Data-Free Self-Improvement in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32770)

*Leon Sick, Lukas Hoyer, Yannick Strümpler, Christian Reisswig, Timo Ropinski, Federico Tombari*

- Model generates its own questions, answers, and reasoning demonstrations via on-policy self-distillation, guided only by coarse topic descriptions.
- No curated prompts, labels, or external teacher.
- Gains across domains while holding performance on untrained domains; works for language benchmarks and VQA.

### 10. [Depth-Dependent Safety Geometry in Vision-Language Models: Layer-Wise Localization and Bidirectional Refusal Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32182)

*Muhammad Zeeshan Karamat, Syed Talal Hassan, Christiana Chamon Garcia*

- Inference-time method to locate and steer safety representations in frozen VLMs.
- Early vision layers allow bidirectional refusal steering; later layers respond to magnitude scaling with a safety-selectivity trade-off; deepest layers separate safe/unsafe but resist steering.
- Similar on InternVL2.5-4B; exposes a white-box refusal-bypass risk in vision encoders.
