# arXiv cs.LG — Top Picks for 2026-10-31

Selected from the 50 cs.LG entries on the first page of the "recent" listing (dated Fri, 30 Oct 2026), focused on alignment/safety, interpretability, generalization, and training dynamics. Slim day for core safety topics; picks lean toward training dynamics and representations.

## Top 10

### 1. [Mode Dynamics: A Framework for Neural Network Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40143)
- Links coordinated parameter changes over a training trajectory to changes in model outputs.
- Decomposes updates into collective modes, tests whether those modes reconstruct behavior change, and measures how concentrated the change is.
- A few leading modes reconstruct behavior; effects are path-dependent; mode count varies by task and data.
- Applied to CIFAR-100 autoencoding and a public Pythia-70M pretraining trajectory.

### 2. [Your LR Schedule May Not Be Principled: Effective Learning Rate Scheduling in Weight-Normalized Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40362)
- Weight-normalized training's gains over weight decay come mainly from its effective-learning-rate (ELR) trajectory, not normalization itself.
- Schedules should target ELR; proposes AIR (affine inverse-sqrt decay) from the theory.
- Beats cosine on 128B-token MoE pretraining (~0.01 lower terminal loss, more robust to hyperparameters).
- Same ELR trajectory reproduces the dynamics under standard weight decay.

### 3. [Training Foundation Models with a Single Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40564)
- Argues pretraining, midtraining, SFT, and RL are not fundamentally separate stages.
- Writes suitable RL objectives as maximum-likelihood fitting to model-generated target distributions, so all stages become one likelihood-based algorithm.
- Single trainer mixes static data with RL samples; improves accuracy-compute tradeoff, preserves broader capabilities, and gives a better starting point for continual learning.

### 4. [Factorized Representations of Concurrent Temporal Variables in Pretrained Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40328)
- Interpretability study of how LMs track two independently changing context variables (language switches and topic changes).
- Both timers are linearly decodable from the same hidden states across ten checkpoints (Qwen3 0.6B–14B).
- Ablating one timer's centroid plane sharply cuts access to it while barely affecting the other; planes are nonorthogonal.
- Representations approximately factorize with additive joint geometry, but no consistent timer-specific causal effect on outputs.

### 5. [Preserving Utility in Continual LLM Unlearning via Orthogonal Projections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40483)
- Sequential deletion requests make existing unlearning methods lose both forgetting and utility.
- COP projects each update orthogonal to activation subspaces cached from earlier forget sets, provably avoiding first-order interference.
- Stores only a low-rank basis per layer; plugs into gradient-based unlearners.
- Better utility and forgetting than continual-unlearning baselines on TOFU and RWKU.

### 6. [SCARL: Sequence Confidence-Aligned Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40407)
- Diagnoses "sequence-confidence misalignment" in RLVR: barely-found successes get the same update as confident ones.
- Reshapes advantages (sign preserved) by the gap between group-relative confidence and outcome, with budget preserved.
- Counters entropy collapse; no extra forward passes, one hyperparameter.
- +2.4 Pass@32 over DAPO on Qwen3-4B-Base; improves 14 of 16 estimator/backbone settings.

### 7. [Learning Calibrated Confidence for Multi-Agent Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39927)
- MASCAL: each agent is RL-trained to output a calibrated probability of being correct and pass it downstream.
- Confidence-aware weighting emphasizes decisions where downstream agents actually respond to upstream confidence.
- Improves accuracy and calibration across three architectures; final confidence enables selective answering and rechecking.

### 8. [One Vector or Many? Clustered Optimal-Transport Steering under Quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40055)
- CHaRS: activation steering using optimal-transport pairing of clusters instead of one difference-of-means vector.
- Proves cluster-size-weighted displacements average to the baseline vector, so gains only come from routing weights.
- Per-token variant reduces toxicity more on Gemma-2-2B and Granite-3.1-2B, but a near-constant map gets ~30% too, so the benefit of per-token treatment is unclear.
- Holds under INT8/NF4 quantization.

### 9. [Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40649)
- Re-examines the claim that random weight perturbations reveal task experts near pretrained LMs.
- A frozen model plus a random bias on output logits matches or beats full-weight perturbation on most reasoning tasks.
- Gains explained by output diversity, validation-based selection, and voting (Perturb-Select-Vote).
- Logit PSV also helps vision under domain shift.

### 10. [Rural Readiness: Domain Generalization with Adversarially Chosen Domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40468)
- Theory: perform on any unseen target domain nearly as well as the worst hypothesis consistent with the k source domains.
- Characterizes sample complexity; relaxing to hypotheses good on 99% of sources cuts it from ~dk to ~d.
- Extends to meta-distributional settings; a small labeled target sample doesn't improve worst-case source sample complexity.

## Honorable Mentions
- [world2vec: Mechanistic world models through latent dynamics learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40637) — reward-free world models that track true dynamics with 10x fewer parameters than DreamerV3/TD-MPC2; aimed at scientific discovery/interpretability.
- [How to Loop Your Transformer: Loop Scaling, Specialization, and Parallel Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40325) — systematic study of looped transformers up to 1B params/155B tokens; per-loop low-rank adapters help, and a fix restores quality for Parallel Loop Transformers.
- [H²R: Hierarchical Hint-Based Reward for Mathematical Reasoning via RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39934) — hint-based reward shaping for RL on math reasoning.
- [The Precision Wall: An Optimizer-Dependent Boundary for Optimization under Power-Law Measurement Spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40485) — optimizer-dependent limits on optimization under power-law spectra.
