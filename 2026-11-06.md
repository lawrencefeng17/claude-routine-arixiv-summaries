# arXiv cs.LG — Top Picks for 2026-11-06

Source: the cs.LG listing for Fri, 6 Nov 2026 (224 entries including cross-lists). Picks are ranked by relevance to alignment, generalization, interpretability, and training dynamics. Summaries are based on the abstracts and the listing. A decent day for monitoring and safety evaluation, and for training dynamics, but nothing directly on emergent misalignment, subliminal learning, or data attribution.

## Top 10

### 1. [Stress Testing Chain-of-Thought Monitoring Against Covert Misalignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07586)
- Asks how robust CoT monitoring is against agents that strategically hide misalignment.
- Introduces TraceMRT, an automated trace-level red-teaming framework. It recasts the side task as a coherent sub-goal of the main task, then searches for reasoning traces that evade the monitor.
- Targets task-specific, contextually grounded evasions that template-based red-teams miss.

### 2. [The Memory Trap: Language Agents Overgeneralize from Benign Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07554)
- Persistent agent memory from benign sessions can cause unsafe or unreliable behavior later.
- Six frontier models were tested. Requests to skip confirmation on low-stakes chores led agents to skip confirmation and verification on simulated high-stakes tasks.
- Memories formed during emotional-support conversations bias later judgments of math, arguments, and poems toward sycophancy.
- A concrete case of benign-data generalization producing misaligned behavior.

### 3. [Compositional Unalignment: Auditing Safety in Large Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07216)
- Uses un-alignment as an audit of how robustly safety is built in, not just a behavioral score.
- Minimal SFT matches the LLM-judge score of a multi-stage RL pipeline, so similar benchmark scores don't establish robustness.
- Recognizing compositional harm and refusing it are decoupled. Nine direction-finding methods detect harm with AUC up to 0.99, yet ablating those directions often leaves refusal unchanged.

### 4. [Mechanistic Shaping & Monitoring: Shaping Models through their Internal Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07071)
- Training-time framework that makes models follow rules about which concepts they express, and which concepts they rely on, using internal representations (e.g. not using gender when screening résumés).
- Works across vision, medical imaging, and language, up to 32B parameters, with classification, SFT, DPO, and GRPO. It reduces targeted behavior while preserving task performance.
- Asks whether shaped concepts stay monitorable after training against a representation, which depends on the class of monitors.

### 5. [Learning Dynamics of On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08016)
- Mechanistic analysis of the OPD feedback loop, where each update changes the student and thus the data that drives later updates.
- Splits the loop into two stages: converting the distillation signal into parameter updates, and broadcasting those updates across prefixes, which reshapes the rollout distribution.
- Explains both limited student responsiveness despite large teacher-student disagreement, and degeneration when local gains in teacher agreement worsen the rollout distribution.

### 6. [Spectral and Subspace Dynamics of LoRA in SFT and RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08414)
- Tracks singular spectra and subspaces of LoRA updates on math reasoning.
- RL concentrates the spectrum at every learning rate, while SFT expands it at moderate rates. RL also rotates subspaces faster at small learning rates.
- Interpolating between SFT and RL isolates group-mean baseline subtraction as the source of both contrasts.
- A quadratic multi-index model explains the effect as a shift in the balance between coherent fitting and gradient noise.

### 7. [Same Answer, Different Future: Matched Predictions Do Not Imply Matched Belief Updating in LMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07913)
- Prepares equal-looking belief states through four textual routes and two activation interventions in a Bernoulli inference task with an analytic Bayes reference.
- Appends identical new evidence and measures how persistent the initial belief is, as an "effective evidence weight".
- Agreement in current predictions doesn't establish equivalent belief states. The route matters for how the model updates later.

### 8. [A Behavioural Framework for Predicting and Understanding Loss of Control in Frontier AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07953)
- Decomposes loss-of-control risk into misaligned motive, harm-enabling capability, and monitoring evasion, measured by 13 aspects and 29 tasks.
- The combined LoC score correlates with failure rates in contained long-horizon risk scenarios across 13 frontier models (r = 0.84).
- Risk varies systematically across models.

### 9. [Towards interpreting any residual stream direction by scaling activation inversion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08465)
- Maximally-Activating Example Metamodels (MAEMs) are LMs that generate text exemplars that strongly activate a given residual-stream direction.
- Trained with a label-free RL objective: cosine similarity between the target direction and the generated text's activations.
- A Qwen3.6-27B MAEM inverts held-out activations better than corpus search or a natural-language autoencoder.

### 10. [Localization Does Not Imply Steerability: A Mechanistic Study of Sign Errors in LLM Mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07478)
- Traces wrong-sign tokens in multi-step math across six open models (14B–675B).
- The sign is committed late, by MLP layers in the last quarter of the network, and this is already present in base models before instruction tuning.
- Mechanisms vary: fixed "minus-stamp" layers, amplifiers of the chosen sign, and a downstream rescue layer in Qwen.
- Finding the circuit does not automatically give a way to steer it.

## Honorable Mentions
- [Understanding AI from the Inside: The State of Mechanistic Interpretability for AI Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08438) — survey asking whether understanding how models work makes them safer.
- [Decodability Is Not Validity: Nuisance Sensitivity in LM Concept Probes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07805) — probes with equal AUROC can differ several-fold in sensitivity to a correlated nuisance attribute.
- [Embedding-Space Fingerprinting of LoRA Fine-Tunes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07610) — the training-data domain of a LoRA fine-tune can be recovered from weights, activations, or outputs alone.
- [Measuring the Marginal Value of Verifier-Based RL Beyond SFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07639) — in a controlled benchmark, GRPO added almost nothing over the base model while SFT nearly solved the tasks.
- [Invariance before structure: two modes of emergent compression in representation learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07952) — separates state-space and parameter-space compression in learned representations.
- [Scaling Laws for SAE Training Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07756), [SAE Interprets MoE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08089), and [An Information Bottleneck for Post-hoc Memorization Mitigation in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07669).
